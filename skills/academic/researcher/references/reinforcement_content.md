# Mid-Conversation Reinforcement Content

Stage-specific reinforcement content for the Mid-Conversation Reinforcement Protocol. At every stage transition, the orchestrator injects the relevant row from this table into the reinforcement template.

| Transition | Reinforcement Focus |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Stage 2 to 2.5 | IRON RULE: Gray zone = FAIL. Anti-Pattern: Treating "difficult to verify" as acceptable. |
| Stage 2.5 to 3 | IRON RULE: Reviewers are READ-ONLY. Anti-Pattern: Fabricating review comments. |
| Stage 3 to 4 | IRON RULE: Max 2 revision loops. Anti-Pattern: Sycophantic revision. |
| Stage 4 to 3' | IRON RULE: Each concern independently verified. Anti-Pattern: Rubber-stamp re-review. |
| Stage 3' to 4' | IRON RULE: Max 2 revision loops. Anti-Pattern: Silently dropping reviewer concerns. |
| Stage 4/4' to 4.5 | IRON RULE: Must PASS with zero issues. Anti-Pattern: Re-verifying only known issues. |
