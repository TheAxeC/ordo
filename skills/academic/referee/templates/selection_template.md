# Selection template

The referee fills this template as `selection.md` at the selection. The section "First-round points resolved" and its count stand only in a second round. Text in `[brackets]` is replaced. The section "Left-out comments" is also written, as it stands, to `left-out.md`.

```markdown
# Selection

## Budget

- Major points: at most [n]
- Minor points: at most [n]
- Length of the report: [n] words
- Direction: [the direction of intake.md]

## Selected points

### Major points

#### M1: [the point, as a phrase]

- Passage: [section, page, line, table or figure, or a short quotation]
- The weakness: [one or two sentences]
- Why it is major: [how it bears on the paper's main claims]
- Status: [second round only: a first-round point not resolved, a first-round point resolved in part, or a new point of the revision]
- Comments merged into this point:
  - [report], [comment number]: [the comment, in a phrase]

### Minor points

#### m1: [the point, as a phrase]

- Passage: [section, page, line, table or figure, or a short quotation]
- The weakness: [one sentence]
- Status: [second round only, as above]
- Comments merged into this point:
  - [report], [comment number]: [the comment, in a phrase]

## Left-out comments

| # | Report | Comment | Why it was left out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [report], [comment number] | [the comment, in a phrase] | [one clause] |

## First-round points resolved

| # | First-round point | How the revision resolved it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [the point, in a phrase] | [one clause] |

## Count

Comments in the reports: [n]. Under a selected point: [n]. Left out: [n]. Resolved first-round points: [n].
```
