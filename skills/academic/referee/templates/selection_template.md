# Selection template

The referee fills this template as `selection.md` at the selection. Text in `[brackets]` is replaced. The sections "Points over the budget" and "Left-out comments" are also written, as they stand, to `left-out.md`.

- The section "Points over the budget" keeps its two headings in every run.
- Each of the two headings reads "None." when the budget cut no point of its grade.
- The section "First-round points resolved" and its count stand only in a second round.
- The lines Status, "First-round point", "Still missing" and "What the revision added or changed" stand only in a second round.
- A point over the budget keeps the number its ranking gives it, after the selected points of its grade.

```markdown
# Selection

## Points found

- Major points the paper has: [n]
- Minor points the paper has: [n]
- Cut by the budget: [n] major points, [n] minor points

## Budget

- Major points: at most [n]
- Minor points: at most [n]
- Length of the report: at most [n] words
- Direction: [the direction of intake.md]

## Selected points

### Major points

[The major points, or "None."]

#### M1: [the point, as a phrase]

- Passage: [section, page, line, table or figure, or a short quotation]
- The weakness: [one or two sentences]
- Why it is major: [the main claim that fails, or cannot be judged, unless the point is settled]
- Status: [a first-round point not resolved, a first-round point resolved in part, or a new point of the revision]
- First-round point: [a remaining first-round point only: its first-round number and title]
- Still missing: [a remaining first-round point only: what the first-round point asked that the revision does not give]
- What the revision added or changed: [a new point only: the material of the revision the defect stands in]
- Comments merged into this point:
  - [report], [comment number]: [the comment, in a phrase]

### Minor points

[The minor points, or "None."]

#### m1: [the point, as a phrase]

- Passage: [section, page, line, table or figure, or a short quotation]
- The weakness: [one sentence]
- Status: [as above]
- First-round point: [as above]
- Still missing: [as above]
- Comments merged into this point:
  - [report], [comment number]: [the comment, in a phrase]

## Points over the budget

### Major points over the budget

#### M8: [the point, as a phrase]

- Passage: [section, page, line, table or figure, or a short quotation]
- The weakness: [one or two sentences]
- Why it is major: [as above]
- Why it is over the budget: [a reason that names the budget, such as "ranked 8 of 10 major points, over the budget of 7"]
- Status: [as above]
- First-round point: [as above]
- Still missing: [as above]
- What the revision added or changed: [as above]
- Comments merged into this point:
  - [report], [comment number]: [the comment, in a phrase]

### Minor points over the budget

- m8: [the point, as a phrase]. Comments: [report], [comment number]; [report], [comment number].

## Left-out comments

| # | Report | Comment | Why it was left out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [report], [comment number] | [the comment, in a phrase] | [one clause] |

## First-round points resolved

| # | First-round point | How the revision resolved it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [its first-round number and title] | [one clause] |

## Count

Comments in the reports: [n]. Under a selected point: [n]. Under a point over the budget: [n]. Left out: [n]. Resolved first-round points: [n].
```
