# Referee report template

The referee fills this template as `referee-report.md`. Text in `[brackets]` is replaced. Each `##` heading is one field of the journal's form, so the user pastes each field from its heading.

The headings below are the default fields. When `intake.md` names the journal's fields, one `##` heading per named field replaces them, in the journal's order, and the content below goes under the field that takes it.

```markdown
# Referee report

## Recommendation

[One value: accept, minor revision, major revision or reject, or one of the journal's values]

## Comments to the author

### General comments

[A summary of the paper: what it claims and on what evidence. Then the overall assessment. In a second round, which first-round points the revision resolved.]

### Major points

1. [The point, tied to its passage: section, page, line, table or figure, or a short quotation. What is wrong, why it matters for the paper's claims, and what would settle it.]

### Minor points

1. [The point, tied to its passage.]

## Confidential comments to the editor

[What the referee tells the editor and not the authors: how sure the recommendation is and what it rests on.]
```
