# Funder config: FWO, postdoctoral fellowship, 2027

An example of a filled funder config, written from two documents of the Research Foundation Flanders (FWO) alone. No run reads it: `/grant config` drafts the config of an application from the call documents the user gives. Each document carries its own year, which "Call" states, so the example is the config of no single call.

## Call

- Funder: Research Foundation Flanders (FWO)
- Programme: Postdoctoral fellowship (junior/senior), fundamental research
- Call year: 2027, the year the score grid is marked with
- Documents read:
  - Score grid: "POSTDOCTORAL FELLOWSHIP FUNDAMENTAL RESEARCH EVALUATION: score grid with scoring descriptors - PRESELECTION", marked "v. 2027", 4 pages.
  - Application form: the form "APPLICATION POSTDOCTORAL FELLOWSHIP (junior/senior)", as printed in the report of an application submitted in December 2024, 30 pages, of which page 10 holds the form's instructions for the project outline and pages 21 to 29 hold the project outline.

The first heading is marked not required since it concerns a resubmission, and the other three are marked required.

## Criteria

- Scale: whole scores from 0 to 7 (Score grid, the header row of pp. 1 to 4).
- Labels: Unacceptable, Weak, Fair/Reasonable, Good/Very good and Excellent/Outstanding, printed in that order in one row under the eight scores, with no boundary between the scores of one label and those of the next (Score grid, pp. 1 to 4). The grid's own sentences on a score are that a score of 5 or higher has a strict requirement for senior postdocs (p. 1) and that criterion 3 needs a minimum score of 4 (p. 4).
- The criteria 1.a and 1.b stand under the criterion "Candidate" (preselection), 2.a and 2.b under the criterion "Project", and 3 under the criterion "Interdisciplinarity", which the grid marks "Specific Interdisciplinary Panel only" (Score grid, pp. 1, 3 and 4).
- For the criterion "Candidate" the grid asks the panel to take into account the candidate's scientific seniority in a junior or senior context and, where appropriate, to allow for mentioned career breaks (Score grid, p. 1).

| Criterion | Descriptors | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1.a. Scientific contribution of the candidate | Assessed: the candidate's scientific contribution to the state of the art, as evidenced by a range of scientifically relevant activities and achievements, such as the quality and impact (rather than the quantity) of the publication record and other meaningful scientific output, and scientific or other impact beyond publications and obtained research funding. Evidence of an emerging scientific reputation and an upward trajectory. The scientific independence of the candidate, which for senior postdocs is a crucial asset and a strict requirement for certain scores. Unacceptable: no basis for evaluation. Weak: rather limited scientific contribution to the state of the art, and little evidence of an upward trajectory. Fair/Reasonable: average scientific contribution to the state of the art. There is some evidence of a starting upward trajectory, or for a senior the earlier upward trajectory is not continuing. Good/Very good: meaningful scientific contribution to the state of the art, properly acknowledged in the scientific community, with an emerging (international) reputation in a clear upward trajectory, and developing scientific independence, which is an asset for junior postdocs and for senior postdocs a strict requirement for a score of 5 or higher. Excellent/Outstanding: impressive scientific contribution through a range of scientifically relevant activities and original, clear achievements beyond the state of the art, an emerging international recognition for the candidate's influential research output, and a proven clear path towards scientific independence, which is an asset for junior postdocs and for senior postdocs a strict requirement for a score of 5 or higher. | Score grid, p. 1 |
| 1.b. Motivation and substantiation of relevant competences of the candidate | Assessed: whether the motivation statement reveals a proper motivation and research vision. The candidate's scientific background and competences, in relation to the proposed project and as required for a postdoctoral researcher in general, and the plan to acquire the proper skills and expertise, verified against the recommendation letter of the supervisor. Further evidence from career and expertise building activities, such as education activities, memberships, networks, research collaborations and research stays, and for senior candidates also supervision, mentoring, involvement as (co-)promotor and institutional responsibilities. Unacceptable: no basis for evaluation. Weak, one or more apply: it is questionable whether an appropriate scientific background and crucial competences are sufficiently present to carry out the proposed project. The candidate shows a limited number of activities to build up a research career and appropriate expertise. Fair/Reasonable, one or more apply: the scientific background and built-up expertise are adequate, except for some flaws that are not all being dealt with in the application. Motivation and vision are present but less pronounced. Good/Very good, all apply: the candidate has a convincing scientific background and expertise to execute the proposed project. The application reveals a strong motivation and research vision regarding the anticipated career development, evidenced by a variety of past and planned activities, skills and experiences. Excellent/Outstanding, all apply: the candidate has an excellent scientific background and expertise, showing the ability and potential to propose and conduct ground-breaking research. The candidate shows a bright, concrete and realistic vision on the own professional future, visible in a wide range of activities, skills and experiences. | Score grid, p. 2 |
| 2.a. Scientific quality, relevance and challenge, originality | Unacceptable, one or more apply: the project is out of scope of the panel it was submitted to (preselection only). The project does not contain real scientific risks or challenges, and there is no contribution to the international state of the art. The project focuses on (economic/societal) valorisation with one stakeholder. Weak, one or more apply: the project proposal is rather a catch-up effort relative to the state of the art. Rather limited level of scientific risks and of pronounced challenges, or challenges not identified. Fair/Reasonable, one or more apply: the added value of the project with respect to the international state of the art is acceptable, but less pronounced or less well elaborated. The project is fairly or reasonably challenging, or it is sufficiently challenging but the potential is insufficiently explored. Good/Very good, all apply: the project is original and soundly builds upon and significantly contributes to the international state of the art. High-quality fundamental research project with a good level of risks, challenges and inventiveness. Excellent/Outstanding, all apply: highly ambitious and original project of potentially groundbreaking nature and large scientific impact. Very high level of scientific risks, with clear inventive and challenging ideas, novel concepts and strategies. | Score grid, p. 3 |
| 2.b. Quality of the research methodology and feasibility of the project | Unacceptable, one or more apply: evident discrepancy or mismatch between the research goals and research methodology. The realization of the scientific goals is not feasible with the proposed research methodology and/or project planning. Weak, one or more apply: the research methodology and project planning are flawed in terms of matching with the project objectives, and the intrinsic feasibility is low. The objectives are formulated in insufficiently concrete terms, making it difficult to evaluate their feasibility. Fair/Reasonable, one or more apply: the research methodology is reasonable but with some shortcomings or a lesser fit to the scientific goals. The feasibility is less realistic, but it is likely that part of the scientific goals will be reached. Good/Very good, all apply: the research methodology and planning are well elaborated and justified, and suitable to reach the targeted scientific objectives, the intrinsic feasibility is good, and risks are identified and dealt with. The project fits well in the research activities of the research group and in the personal development plan of the candidate, as confirmed in the supervisor's recommendation letter. If applicable, required for very good: appropriate measures were taken to make the research transparent and reproducible. Excellent/Outstanding: the requirements of Good/Very good, and a thorough identification of the research risks, with alternative research strategies and fallback research options. | Score grid, p. 3 |
| 3. Level of interdisciplinarity | Assessed, in the Specific Interdisciplinary panel only: to what extent the application is interdisciplinary, taking the project proposal, the profile of the candidate and the research group or groups into account. A minimum score of 4 is necessary to receive funding from the Specific Interdisciplinary Panel. Unacceptable: the project is not interdisciplinary or multidisciplinary at all, and the proposed research is focused within one discipline. Weak: the project is multidisciplinary instead of interdisciplinary in nature. The research covers at least two disciplines, but one discipline is merely used as an instrument for the other, the domains are juxtaposed, and the outcomes are not likely to impact all involved disciplines. Fair/Reasonable, one or more apply: some characteristics of interdisciplinarity are present, but not all requirements of Good/Very good are met, for example when the disciplines are located in the same FWO panel. Mutual interactive input is necessary from at least two distinct disciplines, but the level of coordination and integration is insufficiently extensive or profound. The involved disciplines do not sufficiently influence one another and do not benefit to the same extent from the project. Good/Very good, all apply: there is more than one discipline involved in the proposed project, and these disciplines are sufficiently distinct. The disciplines are at a similar coordinated level and each discipline is essential to achieve the expected outcome. The state of the art is advanced in all involved disciplines and/or in a shared area. Excellent/Outstanding: the requirements of Good/Very good, a pronounced synergy between all involved disciplines, that strongly benefit from and mutually influence each other in an integrated and well-designed way, and outcomes that will clearly impact all involved disciplines, so that there is substantial added value for each involved discipline and/or new bridges between previously rarely related fields are built or new subdisciplines could result from this project. | Score grid, p. 4 |
