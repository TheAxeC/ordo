## What to build

One new skill folder, `skills/academic/referee/`, and the places that list a skill. When the step is done, a user who types `/referee <manuscript>` gets one referee report on someone else's paper, written by one reviewer from the reviewer's side for the editor, and `/referee re-review ...` gives the second-round report.

The skill is small. It holds only what a referee adds. Every reviewer runs from the pages of `paper-review-panel`, named as the layout names another skill's file. No page of `paper-review-panel`, and no part of one longer than a sentence, is copied into `skills/academic/referee/`. No file under `skills/academic/paper-review-panel/` is changed.

### The files of the skill

1. `skills/academic/referee/SKILL.md`, in the layout of `docs/dev/skill-layout.md`: the frontmatter, then the nine sections in order.
   - `name: referee`, `metadata.version: "1.0.0"`.
   - `description` says what the skill does and leaves behind, then `Triggers on:` with `/referee` and the phrases for refereeing someone else's paper, for a first round and for a revision. It names no neighbouring skill and is at most 1,024 characters.
   - The opening paragraph says it is the referee review of the glossary and that it is neither a draft review nor a panel review.
   - "Use instead" names `/paper-review-panel` for the review of the author's own paper by a panel, and `/paper draft-review` for the review of the author's own draft.
2. `skills/academic/referee/agents/referee_agent.md`: the page of the one role the skill adds, the referee. It holds what the role does in its two calls: the selection, and the report.
3. `skills/academic/referee/references/second_round.md`: what a second-round run does, read only by a run of `re-review`.
4. `skills/academic/referee/templates/selection_template.md`: the template the referee fills at the selection, with the selected comments and the left-out comments.
5. `skills/academic/referee/templates/referee_report_template.md`: the template of the report, one heading per field of the journal, with the default fields.

### The behaviour, first round (`/referee <manuscript>`)

Each of these is a step of `SKILL.md`, ending on its completion criterion, in this order.

1. The call is read. The manuscript is a path or pasted text.
   - The folder `paper-review-panel` must stand beside the skill's own folder, with its `SKILL.md`. When it does not, the run is refused before anything is written: the refusal says that `referee` runs the pages of the `paper-review-panel` skill and that this skill is not installed beside it.
   - A call with no manuscript is a refusal.
2. The run folder is made (Rules below).
3. The intake, a stop, asked in one message before any agent starts. It asks:
   - the direction, in the user's words, such as a methodological focus; with none given the direction is "balanced";
   - the severity, in the user's words; with none given it is "medium";
   - the budget: the most major points, the most minor points, and the length of the report in words; the default is 7 major points, 7 minor points and 1,200 words;
   - the journal's fields and its length limit, when it has one; with no fields named, the fields are the default ones of the report template;
   - whether to shrink the panel, and to which reviewers; with no answer all five reviewers run;
   - the round, only for a plain request that does not say it.
   - The answers are written to `intake.md` in the run folder.
4. The field analyst is started from the `paper-review-panel` skill's `agents/field_analyst_agent.md`, given the manuscript and the intake's direction, so the Reviewer Configuration Card carries the direction in each reviewer's focus. Then the Reviewer Configuration Card is shown to the user, who confirms or adjusts it, as the `paper-review-panel` skill's "Steps", "full", items 1 and 2 say.
5. The reviewers are started at once, each from its page of the `paper-review-panel` skill, as that skill's "Steps", "full", item 3 says.
   - All five run under the full contract, in two calls each ("The full contract" in the `paper-review-panel` skill's `SKILL.md`).
   - A panel shrunk at the intake to the EIC and the methodology reviewer runs under the methodology-focus contract, in two calls each.
   - Any other shrunk panel runs each of its reviewers in one call, with no contract.
   - The stop `[PANEL-SHRUNK]` of the `paper-review-panel` skill holds here as there: a reviewer marked unusable under a contract ends the run.
6. The editorial synthesizer is not started, no Revision Roadmap is written, and no coaching dialogue is held.
7. The selection. The referee is started as a fresh agent, given `agents/referee_agent.md`, the manuscript, `intake.md`, every reviewer's report and `templates/selection_template.md`. It writes `selection.md` to the run folder:
   - every comment of every report is listed once, either as selected or as left out;
   - comments about the same weakness are merged into one point before anything is left out, and the point names each report it came from;
   - a point is major when it bears on whether the paper's main claims hold; a point raised by several reviewers is kept ahead of one raised once; the direction of the intake weights the choice;
   - the selected points are within the budget of the intake: no more major points and no more minor points than it gives;
   - each selected point is tied to the passage it is about: the section, page, line, table or figure, or a short quotation;
   - a comment merged into a selected point counts as selected and is listed under that point, never among the left-out comments;
   - each left-out comment has one clause saying why it was left out, such as minor against the budget, outside the direction, or a matter of taste.
8. The selection stop. The session shows the user the selected points and the left-out comments. The user confirms, or moves items between the two lists, or changes a point from major to minor or back. The session writes the user's changes into `selection.md`. A selection the user has pushed past the budget is kept as the user made it.
9. The report. The same referee agent is resumed with the confirmed `selection.md` and `templates/referee_report_template.md`, and writes `referee-report.md` to the run folder:
   - one heading per field of the journal; by default a recommendation, the comments to the author (general comments with a summary of the paper, then the major points and the minor points, numbered) and the confidential comments to the editor;
   - written from the selected points only: no left-out comment is in it;
   - in one voice, the first person singular of one reviewer; it names no panel, no reviewer role, no count of reviewers and no agreement between reviewers;
   - from the reviewer's side, for the editor and the authors of someone else's paper: it holds no revision roadmap and no coaching;
   - each point is tied to its passage, as in the selection;
   - at the severity of the intake, and within the length of the budget and the journal's limit;
   - the recommendation is one of the journal's values when the user gave them at the intake, and otherwise one of accept, minor revision, major revision, reject;
   - the confidential comments to the editor say what the referee would tell the editor and not the authors, such as how sure the recommendation is and what it rests on, and they repeat no point of the comments to the author word for word.
10. The run ends by naming `referee-report.md` and the file of the left-out comments.

The left-out comments are kept in `left-out.md` in the run folder, each with its reason. The referee writes it at the selection, and the session rewrites it after the selection stop so that it holds exactly the comments not selected.

### The behaviour, second round (`/referee re-review <revised manuscript> <response letter> [<the first-round comments>]`)

The steps of the first round stand in `SKILL.md` under the subsection `### first round`. `SKILL.md` has a `### re-review` subsection whose steps point at `references/second_round.md`, which holds:

1. The inputs: the revised manuscript and the authors' response letter are required, and a call that lacks one is a refusal. The referee's first-round comments are optional; without them the list of first-round points is read from the points the response letter quotes or answers.
2. The intake, as in the first round, except that the question on shrinking the panel is replaced by the question whether all five reviewers run again; with no answer they do not.
3. The check of the revision. The field analyst and the EIC are started from their pages of the `paper-review-panel` skill, and the EIC checks each first-round point against the revised manuscript and the response as that skill's `references/re_review_mode_protocol.md` gives it in "Verification Logic", "Commitment Ledger Verification" and "New Issue Detection", the first-round points standing where that page has the Revision Roadmap. The EIC runs in one call, with no contract. The Reviewer Configuration Card is shown and confirmed as in the first round. The editorial synthesizer is not started, and the Socratic guidance of that page is not held.
4. When the user asked for all five at the intake, the five reviewers also review the revised manuscript as in the first round.
5. The selection, the selection stop and the report, as in the first round, from the EIC's check and any reviewer reports. Each selected point says whether it is a first-round point not resolved, one resolved in part, or a new point of the revision. The report says, in its general comments, which first-round points the revision resolved.

### The rules of the skill

- The run folder is `referee-runs/<round>-<date>/` in the manuscript's folder, `<round>` being `round-1` for a first round and `round-2` for `re-review`, and `<date>` the local date, `date +%Y-%m-%d`. For pasted text it stands in the folder the session runs in. A further run of the same round on the same day takes `-2`, then `-3`.
- Each output is a file of its own in the run folder: `intake.md`, the Reviewer Configuration Card, each reviewer's report with the outputs of its calls, `selection.md`, `left-out.md` and `referee-report.md`.
- Each role starts as a fresh agent on the runner's general-purpose agent with the runner's defaults, given its page and the files of its task; the prompt sets no model and no effort.
- The read-only rule and the rule on untrusted review materials of the `paper-review-panel` skill's Rules hold for every agent of a run, and `SKILL.md` names them by that section instead of restating them at length.
- The manuscript is someone else's: no output is addressed to its author as the author's own adviser.

The Stops table has the rows: the intake, the Reviewer Configuration, the selection, `[PANEL-SHRUNK]`, and the refusals (the panel skill not installed, a missing input). The Anti-patterns table has at least these: carrying every comment of the panel into the report; a report that shows the panel; a copy of a page of `paper-review-panel` in this skill; writing a revision roadmap or coaching the author.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The skill adds one role, the referee, with one page, started once for the selection and resumed for the report, so the report is written by the agent that made the selection.
2. The selection replaces the panel's editorial synthesis: the editorial synthesizer is not started in either round.
3. The direction and the severity are taken in the user's own words, with the defaults "balanced" and "medium", so the skill fixes no list of values.
4. The default budget is 7 major points, 7 minor points and 1,200 words, the upper end of the ruled "about 5 to 7 major points, about 7 minor points and about 1,200 words".
5. A point is major when it bears on whether the paper's main claims hold, comments on one weakness are merged before any is left out, and a point several reviewers raised is kept ahead of one raised once.
6. A shrunk panel other than the EIC with the methodology reviewer runs without a contract, since `paper-review-panel` has a contract only for a panel of five and for that pair.
7. The report is one file, `referee-report.md`, with one heading per field of the journal, so each field is pasted into the journal's form from its heading.
8. The run folder's `<round>` is `round-1` or `round-2`, and the folder stands in the manuscript's folder, as the ruling "beside the manuscript" says.
9. In the second round the first-round points stand where the re-review protocol has the Revision Roadmap, and without the first-round comments they are read from the response letter.
10. No file of `paper-review-panel` is changed by this step: whether its `SKILL.md` names `referee` under "Use instead", and whether the phrase "referee report" stays among its triggers, is the user's decision, Open item 1 of the state file.
11. `skills/academic/paper/SKILL.md` takes a patch version for the removed words.
