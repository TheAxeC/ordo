## What to build

1. **`skills/spec/templates/check_step.py` and `check_step.test.sh`.** `check_step.py <plan.md> <step>` reads the "Steps, in execution order" section and the Rulings section of `plan.md`. It exits 0 with one `ok:` line when the step's line ends with `(approved)` or with one or more `(ruling <name>)` tags of which at least one names a ruling line of the Rulings section that ends with "(the user)" in either case. It exits 1 with one line naming the step and the reason when the step's line has no tag, names only rulings the Rulings section lacks, or starts with `Removed by`. It exits 64 on a missing file, a missing section, or a step not in the list, printing the list. A ruling's name is its open item letter or letters for an `- Open item <L> (` line, and the text before ` (` for any other ruling line. The test covers each outcome on scratch files, each case naming the revert that turns it red.
2. **`/spec` runs it.** `skills/spec/SKILL.md` Steps: before any premise check, run `python3 templates/check_step.py <plan.md> <step>`; exit 1 is a refusal that names the step and says the user's ruling is needed, and it writes nothing. The Stops table gets the row. "What it reads" names the step's authority.
3. **`/plan` writes the tags.** `skills/plan/SKILL.md` Steps 3: once the user approves the list, each step line ends with `(approved)`; a step added later ends with `(ruling <name>)` of the ruling that added it, and the Rules say so. `skills/plan/templates/plan.md` shows the form on its example step lines. `plan-orchestration/SKILL.md`, where it books a ruling that adds or splits a step, says the new line carries `(ruling <name>)`; `skills/spec/SKILL.md`'s "When it stopped" booking says the same.
4. **`/land` requires the ledger's `land.sh`.** `skills/land/SKILL.md`: "The landing script" says a ledger holds `land.sh`, copied from `templates/land.sh` with its `ADAPT` edits made, and `/land` refuses (a Stops row) when the ledger has none. `/plan` copies `templates/land.sh` and `templates/land.test.sh` into the ledger when it opens a plan, with the `ADAPT` edits made from `.agents/plan.yaml`.
5. **`land.sh` usable in any repository.** Every project-specific step of `skills/land/templates/land.sh` sits inside an `ADAPT` place with an empty default: the "source line counts" step and the browser step (with its port check and `npm run test:browser`) move into the `ADAPT` block, or become `ADAPT` settings whose default runs nothing. `landing_tool_path` defaults to `.`, the whole tree.
