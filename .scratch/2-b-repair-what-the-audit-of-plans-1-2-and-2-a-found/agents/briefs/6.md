## What to build

1. **Numbered findings.** A finding is a top-level `- ` item or a `<n>. ` or `<n>) ` item, with its indented continuation lines, under one of the four headings.
2. **Subheadings in a round.** Inside `## Repair round <n>, refuted`, the `### Spec`, `### Proof`, `### Standards`, `### Behaviour` subheadings (in either case, with or without a leading number such as `### 1. Spec`) give each finding its heading and run.
3. **Sections that are not findings.** `Verification`, `Verification lines`, `Not checked`, `Closed`, `Closures`, `Usage` and any section whose items are closures (the round's "Closures checked" style) are skipped, at `##` and `###` level.
4. **Fences.** Both backtick and tilde fences, of any length of three or more, are skipped until their closing fence.
5. **`--exclude-listed`.** The paths are read from the backtick-quoted entries under the earlier retro's "## Reports read" heading and compared by `os.path.realpath`, so a path with a space or spelled another way is excluded.
6. **Tests.** `skills/plan-retro/templates/collect_findings.test.sh` gains fixtures in the shapes the archived reports use (numbered findings under `##` headings; a `## Repair round 1, refuted` section with `###` subheadings, a `### Not checked` list and a closures paragraph; a tilde fence holding a heading and a bullet; a report path holding a space for `--exclude-listed`), each case red under the revert that undoes its item. The head comment names the cases.
7. **The count.** The report gives the collector's count over `.scratch/archive` after the change and a count by hand for at least three archived refuter reports of different shapes (one of plan 1, one of plan 2, one of plan 2.A), each with the command that counted, and the two agree.
8. **README.md:119** says what the test covers.
