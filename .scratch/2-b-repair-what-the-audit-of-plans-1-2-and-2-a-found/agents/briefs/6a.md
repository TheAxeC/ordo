## What to build

In `skills/plan-retro/templates/collect_findings.py` and its test:

1. **The no-finding forms.** "none" keeps its breadth: an item opening with the word "none" reports nothing. "nothing", "no finding", "no findings", "no defect", "no defects", "otherwise none" and "checked, no defect(s)" report nothing only when they are the item's whole first sentence, optionally followed by "found" or "here". The "reproduce" form (`OTHERS_REPRODUCE`) is unchanged.
2. **Closures.** A round's item is a closure that holds when it opens with "Closed" followed by the end of the sentence or a `.`, `;` or `:`, or with "<label>: closed" followed by the end or a `.` or `;`, or with "Closures checked, all hold" or "Checked and holding"; and its first clause (up to the first `.` or `;`) holds none of the words "not", "no", "only", "still", "partly", "in part" and no `?`. Every other item stays a finding.
3. **Tests.** Every case of "Cases" above is a fixture item, asserted as the section says. A fixture heading that combines two suffixes (for example `## Behaviour (none found):`) gives its findings the heading `behaviour`. `--exclude-listed` refuses an entry whose plan is `.`, one whose file name does not end `-refuter.md`, and one whose step is empty, each with exit 2. Each case red under the revert that undoes what it checks (the old `NOTHING_FOUND`, the old `CLOSURE`, a single pass in `heading_name`, each removed entry check).
4. **The docstring** states the forms of decisions 1 and 2, and the test's head comment names the new cases.
5. **The counts.** The report gives `python3 -B skills/plan-retro/templates/collect_findings.py .scratch .scratch/archive | wc -l` and the same over `.scratch/archive`, before and after, and the hand counts of step 6 (the four archived reports of `6-report.md`, "The hand count") given again against the new collector, each difference explained by item.
6. **The complete list.** The report lists every item in the collector's output over `.scratch .scratch/archive` that reports no defect, read row by row, with its report, run, heading and text, and the command that produced the rows. These items stay findings of the collector; `plan-retro` sets them aside as the kind "no defect".

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The whole-first-sentence rule of item 1, with "found" or "here" allowed after the form, so a sentence that goes on to name a defect stays a finding.
2. The closure rule of item 2, with the negation words as listed.
3. Items that report no defect in other wordings stay findings; the retro sets them aside by reading.
