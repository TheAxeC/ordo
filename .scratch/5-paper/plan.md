## Goal

The paper skill, built on academic-paper and run as `/paper <mode>` under academic-paper's mode names on a LaTeX manuscript and its `.bib`: drafting, structure, citations, with the claim check against each source's full text in full, revision and citation-check mode, figures and statistics, the AI disclosure, CRediT, funding and ethics statements, revision patches and the lit-review mode with its literature strategist, which writes a BibTeX entry for each included source with `lookup.py bibtex`, as the coverage inventory marks them rebuild, the rows it marked rebuild later included (plan mode and format conversion among them), with the manuscript scripts `anchorize_tex.py` and `apply_tex_patch.py` moved in from `research-hub/tools/manuscript` and `lookup.py` with its test taken from commit 0074728 for checking every reference the skill keeps, every other reference lookup and the `\cite` key check. It applies the `/writing` findings you accept to the manuscript, or has its draft writer judge and apply them when you ask. Its design follows academic-paper's (research-hub's copy, 3.2.0): the agents, phases, cycles and stops are kept as the source has them, and each deviation from them is made only with its reason and your ruling in the plan's Rulings.
