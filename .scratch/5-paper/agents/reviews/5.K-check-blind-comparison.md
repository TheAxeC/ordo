## What the judges held against `paper`

Each point names the judges that raised it; the place is in the `paper` output.

- The origin of PARAFAC2 is missing: Harshman 1972 was excluded by the screening rule on age and citations, and Smilde 1997 and Bro, Smilde and de Jong 2001 as not confirmed (`final-message.md:46`); `grep -ci harshman refs.bib` prints 0. Both academic-paper runs include Harshman. Judges 1, 2, 3, 4.
- Gap 5 says lesion-set cohorts "range from 83 to 831 patients in the sources found" (`review.md:2396`), while the run's own `geady2024multimetastatic` has 3,930 patients (`review.md:62`, `:1944`). Judges 1, 2, 3.
- `Huang2025CyclicShiftSKPD` is cited as a preprint (`refs.bib:259`, `@misc`) where the Statistics and Computing 2026 version exists. Judges 1, 2, 3, 4.
- The synthesis is short (`review.md:2398-2410`), groups citations with few effect sizes or cohort sizes. Judges 1, 2, 3.
- The registration side is thin: nothing on registration at lesions, cost-function masking or lesion-symptom mapping (`grep -ci` of "cost-function mask" and "lesion-symptom" prints 0 in `review.md`), and registration-free transport fusion in medical imaging (the prostate MRI study academic-paper cites) is not covered. Judges 1, 2.
- Sources added to pass the currency gate, some rated Low and "kept here for the currency of the merged set" (`review.md:1425`, `:2194`), and pathology foundation models of weak relevance. Judges 2, 4.
- Theme 2, the optimal transport theme, was annotated from abstracts and landing pages only (`final-message.md:48`). Judges 3, 4.
- Fawaz et al. 2021, which measures what registration changes, is missing (`grep -ci fawaz refs.bib` prints 0), and gap 2 says no such comparison exists. Judge 4.
- The gaps name only which theme runs found nothing, not the queries and counts behind each null search. Judges 2, 4.
- The search screened 1,628 records against academic-paper's 16,808, and three themes defaulted to the last 10 years. Judge 3.
- `[PROFILE-DISCIPLINE-MISMATCH]` stands in the deliverable (`review.md:11`). Judge 2.

## What the judges credited `paper` with

No claim found false against a source wherever the judges checked, apart from gap 5; a readable review; broader theory coverage (set-function theory, Janossy pooling, kernel distribution regression, Wagstaff 2022); the framing of the methods by what each subject is compared with (`review.md:2410`); a currency gate applied on the strict 2022 to 2026 window; and broader medical-imaging coverage than step 5's academic-paper run (autoPET, TotalSegmentator, Learn2Reg, foundation-model encoders).
