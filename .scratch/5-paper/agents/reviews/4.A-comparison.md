## Contradictions inside paper

1. Showing the mentor's message at the handoff. `SKILL.md`, plan item 6, shows "`chapter-plan.md` with its gate and the INSIGHT collection of `insights.md`". Item 2 maps `next: chapter plan` straight to item 5. Read by the items, the observation never reaches the user.
2. The pause message. `socratic-mentor.md`, "Limits": on a stop it "ends with `next: pause`. It tells the user the run can be continued later". In `SKILL.md`, only messages ending `next: question`, `next: outline` or `next: evaluate` are shown (item 2; the Stops table row "The mentor's question"). Item 7 writes the session's own final message to `paused.md`, so the mentor's `next: pause` and `next: outline-only` messages are never shown.
