## What the judges held against `paper`

Each point names the judges that raised it; the place is in the `paper` output as the judges quote it.

- The question's parts are numbered two ways: theme run 4 numbered P1 to P3 differently, and its annotations keep its own numbering (`review.md:15`). Judges 1, 2, 3, 4.
- The annotations and the matrix do not record the question's parts per source; academic-paper's carry a field each for correspondence handling, the relation to the pipelines and whether the method is supervised, and its matrix has those columns. Judges 2, 3, 4.
- The gaps name sources but not the searches that found nothing; academic-paper's name the null searches by query and count. Judges 1, 2, 4.
- The synthesis is shorter and mostly lists sources by family; one "divergent finding" sets two results that do not conflict against each other (`review.md:2693`), and one sentence sets a lesion count against an F1 score (`review.md:2681`). Judges 1, 2.
- Harshman 1972 (PARAFAC2) is reported as having no index record and no readable copy (`review.md:2728`), while a copy is at `psychology.uwo.ca/faculty/harshman/wpppfac2.pdf`, which academic-paper cites. Judges 3, 4.
- Two sources included without their abstracts read and seven with Key results left GAP (`review.md:2750`, `:2755`). Judges 2, 3, 4.
- The AI disclosure says "A person oversaw the process" (`review.md:2759`), which no person did; the sentence is the template of `skills/paper/references/ai-disclosure.md:189`. Judge 3.
- Tool names and run records in the review body (the lookup script's calls, "node 4 of the screening tree"). Judges 1, 2.
- The medical-imaging core of transport-based morphometry and registration-free fusion (Kundu, Gong's CMOT-Fusion) is missing. Judge 2.
- The Thual TMLR version kept as a preprint after a title ratio of 0.42. Judges 1, 4.
- The per-entry confirmation stated for all 225 entries rests on the merge run's report, not a rerun. Judge 4.
