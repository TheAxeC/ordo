### Strengths
1. The method now holds the survival design, and the Results open on a result. 4.2: "Each patient's risk in the survival design came from the fold in which that patient was held out. The clinical Cox model was fitted on the same training folds as the networks." 5.2, second paragraph: "In the survival design, the same cross-validated risks were read at every horizon without refitting (Table~\ref{tab:horizons})." The three-network mean is stated once, in 3.2.
2. The Results now carry the comparison the design implication rests on. 5.2: "It also lay above the interval of ECG-only BTTN, 0.7397 [0.7275, 0.7517]". The values equal the saved one-year intervals of the fused model and ECG-only BTTN, and the Conclusion's third paragraph now repeats a result stated in 5.2.
3. The exclusion count is stated with its criterion where it stands. Table 1 caption: "Prevalent or concurrent AF counts the patients with an AF diagnosis, or an ECG read as AF, before the end of the 30-day blanking window after the index ECG. These patients were excluded, and the count is not an outcome." This agrees with 4.1 and with the exclusion rule of the analysis (an AF onset, or a first AF-read ECG, at or before 30 days after the index ECG).
4. The two cited sentences now say what their sources say. 1.1: "Over 50 years of the Framingham Heart Study, the age-adjusted prevalence of AF rose about fourfold and its incidence more than tripled", against the source's "Framingham Heart Study participants (n=9511) from 1958 to 2007". 6.1: "a foundation model reported an AUROC of 0.996 for the AF label of multi-label ECG interpretation, and its authors released an open benchmark on MIMIC-IV-ECG", against the abstract's "Downstream, we evaluate multi-label ECG interpretation and prediction of reduced left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF), introducing an openly available benchmark on the MIMIC-IV-ECG dataset" and "achieve high AUROC on salient labels, including atrial fibrillation (0.996)".
5. The Discussion keeps the size of the gain apart from its uncertainty. 6.1: "The gain of the fused model over the clinical model is small and consistent in direction. The one-year intervals overlap, and the study reports no interval of the difference that would establish it."
