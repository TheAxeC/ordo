## What the judges held against `paper`

Each point names the judges that raised it; the place is in the `paper` output.

- Literatures the request names are missing: no segmentation method or benchmark (U-Net, nnU-Net, BraTS), one registration method (SyN, `AVANTS_2008`), no learned registration, no unbalanced or partial transport (Chizat, Sejourne), no N-way PLS (Bro 1996), no Gromov-Wasserstein dictionaries; `refs.bib` holds none of nnU-Net, VoxelMorph, Chizat, Bro 1996, Sarasua or Fawaz (`grep -c` printed 0). Judges 1, 2, 3, 4.
- The merge run's merit rule cut central works as "older than 2022 that are neither must-include nor seminal" (OTKE 2021, OGDL 2021, Gramfort 2015, Bazeille 2019, PointNet++, Guhaniyogi 2017) and as relevance 2 (Thual 2022, "alignment of brains with no outcome") (`review.md:141-147`). The run set no time range at intake, and step 5's answer gives none. Judges 1, 3, 4.
- Gap MG2 ("no study compares registration-based and registration-free methods on the same cohort and outcome", `review.md:1796`) is false: Sarasua et al. 2022 and Fawaz et al. 2021 do so, and the run found neither. Judges 3, 4.
- Karimzadeh 2026's AUC of 0.91 is stated as reached "with an external centre" (`review.md:1830`); the source gives it for internal cross-validation on centre 1 and reports a domain shift at the external centre. Judges 1, 4.
- `refs.bib` entries left with defects the final message lists: an empty author name (`Liu_2023a`), `&amp;` in five entries, "and others" in three. Judges 1, 2, 3, 4.
- The synthesis is shorter and mostly lists sources by family. Judges 1, 2.
- The currency gate fails: 46.7 percent from 2022 to 2026. Judges 1, 2.
- Many entries on histopathology multiple-instance learning and on EHR tensors, tangential to unaligned spatial modes. Judge 2.
