## What the judges held against `paper`

Each point names the judges that raised it; the place is in the `paper` output.

- Transport-based morphometry (Kundu 2018, 2020, 2024; Ironside 2025) and CMOT-Fusion (Gong 2026) are missing, the main clinical use of transport predictors and the clearest case of registration-free transport fusion the request names. Judges 1, 2.
- Gap 1 (`review.md:1904`), "no included transport study predicts a clinical outcome", is true only because of those omissions and conflicts with Section 1.4 (`review.md:1980`, FlowLOT, Ma 2023). Judges 1, 2.
- Canonical pipeline works are missing: VoxelMorph, LDDMM, SyN, U-Net, nnU-Net, and the whole-slide MIL papers (Campanella, CLAM, TransMIL); judge 4 also names Tucker 1966 and Sarasua 2022, the one same-cohort comparison of registered and unaligned inputs. Judges 1, 2, 3, 4.
- The currency gate passes at exactly 50.0 percent only by counting an entry Crossref dates 2027 (`review.md:1946`). Judges 1, 2, 4.
- About 45 sources read at abstract level only, and four named databases not searched (disclosed). Judges 2, 3, 4.
- Gap 2 (`review.md:1906`) says AMINN pools with attention; its source says average pooling performs best, as the annotation at `review.md:1186` states. Judge 3.
- Stance tags defined per strand (`review.md:183-188`), so they cannot be compared across the corpus. Judge 4.
- `kossaifi2017tensor` given as a preprint with no peer-reviewed version; `luo2022prediction` dated 2022 where the volume is 2023. Judge 3.
