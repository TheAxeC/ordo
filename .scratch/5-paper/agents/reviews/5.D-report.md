## Judgment calls the brief left open

1. **A published DOI that does not match.** A DOI whose record is below 0.70 prints `published DOI <doi>: title ratio <r> against <index>, below 0.70`, and a DOI no index holds prints `published DOI <doi>: not found` with a left-out line for each index left out; the next published DOI is then tried. 2. **The "published in" line after a rejected DOI.** When a DOI was named and none was taken, the line's clause reads "with no DOI named by Semantic Scholar or OpenAlex whose record matches the title", so that it is not false; with no DOI named it is the brief's text exactly (V1).
3. **The "published in" line after a settled DOI.** When doi.org gave no entry, or every index was left out, the line is not printed, as the brief's "Otherwise" orders the branches (V5, V6).
