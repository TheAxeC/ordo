## Judgment calls the brief left open

1. **A published DOI that does not match.** A DOI whose record is below 0.70 prints `published DOI <doi>: title ratio <r> against <index>, below 0.70`, and a DOI no index holds prints `published DOI <doi>: not found` with a left-out line for each index left out; the next published DOI is then tried. The brief names no line for these, and without one the agent cannot tell why the `@misc` entry came back.
2. **The "published in" line after a rejected DOI.** When a DOI was named and none was taken, the line's clause reads "with no DOI named by Semantic Scholar or OpenAlex whose record matches the title", so that it is not false; with no DOI named it is the brief's text exactly (V1).
3. **The "published in" line after a settled DOI.** When doi.org gave no entry, or every index was left out, the line is not printed, as the brief's "Otherwise" orders the branches (V5, V6).
4. **`--page` with no record to compare.** With arXiv and Semantic Scholar both giving no title, `--page` prints the arXiv line and `page <url>: no record of arXiv <id> to compare its title with`, exit 1.
5. **`--page` with a DOI that is not an arXiv DOI** is a call error, `error: --page needs an arXiv ID`, exit 2, listed in the head comment.
6. **The venue of a page entry** comes from `citation_conference_title`, else `citation_journal_title`; a page with neither gives an entry with no `booktitle` or `journal` field, since the brief names only the tags.
7. **An address on doi.org or arXiv** is refused before any request, for `--page`, `resolve --entry` and `fetch --page` alike; an address that redirects there is refused after the redirect.
8. **A page that passes and no record that agrees.** `resolve --entry` then leaves the NOT_CONFIRMED line as it is, with no suffix, since every page check passed.
9. **"has no `doi`"** is read as a `doi` field that is not a DOI; a `doi` of the form `10.48550/arXiv.<id>` is a DOI, so such an entry is resolved by it as now and no page is read.
10. **`fetch --page` lines.** `page <url>: citation_pdf_url <address>`, `page <url>: <reason>` and `page <url>: no citation_pdf_url tag`; `--page` is taken with any input of `fetch`.
11. **The database table's ways.** EBSCO and ProQuest databases (Education Source, MLA International Bibliography, Business Source Complete, ABI/INFORM) are "not reached", since they have no public interface and no public record pages; Google Scholar is "not reached" (no API; a `curl` probe on 2026-11-16 got a 302 to `www.google.com/sgsync`).
