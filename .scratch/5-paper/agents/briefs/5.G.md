## What to build

1. **The theme search, in `skills/paper/SKILL.md` and `references/literature-strategist.md`.** In full, outline-only and lit-review mode, the search for the review runs as the ruling The theme search says.
   - **The search themes.** Before it starts the strategist for the review, the session divides the topic and the research question into search themes, one for each research community the request reaches: a field with its own literature, venues and terms, such as tensor regression, optimal transport, set encoders and medical image pipelines in step 5's request. A request that stays inside one community has one search theme. The session writes the search themes in `config.md` under "Decisions": each one's name, the strands of the request it covers, its source-count range (decision 2), and the reason for the division. This is a choice of the session, not a stop.
   - **Two levels of theme.** A search theme is a level above the themes of the literature matrix. The matrix keeps its topical themes as its columns, as now, and each matrix row also names the search theme or themes that found the source. Condition 3 of the pass rule, the matrix coverage gate, the theme divisions the report suggests and the planner read the matrix's themes, as now. The pages name the new level "search theme" every time, so no sentence on the tree that says "theme" changes its sense.
   - **The theme runs.** With two search themes or more, the session starts one literature strategist per search theme, all together (Steps 8). Each is given everything the single strategist is given now, plus its search theme and its range, and its own subfolder `theme-<k>/` of the run's temporary folder. Each runs the whole page for its search theme: the parts of the research question that fall in it, the search design, the four searches with their passes and their end, the screening, the annotation of each source it includes, the `lookup.py` checks of every entry it writes, and its gates, with its range in place of the paper type's minimum. Its record lists the sources it cut at its range's upper bound and the entries it took out as not confirmed. A theme run that ends below its range's lower bound says so in its record, and the merge run's gates handle the whole set.

     - Its search record gives each theme run's databases, passes, records screened and sources kept, then the merge run's own searches and counts, and the duplicates removed. Its "No entry written" list holds every entry a theme run or the merge run took out as not confirmed, and the final message names them, as lit-review item 1 says now.
