## What to build

1. In `skills/roadmap/SKILL.md`, "What it reads" 6, replace the two sub-bullets "Such a bullet names its finding by ..." and "`/roadmap` reads that report and finds the finding there." with these five, at the same indent, in this order, word for word:

   ```
   - Such a bullet names its finding by the path of its report under the plan's `agents/reviews/` (a refuter report, a brief-check report, a landing report or a diagnosis record) and the heading the finding stands under, written as the report writes it.
   - For a refuter report or a brief-check report, the bullet also gives the finding's number: its place, counted from 1, in the list of findings under that heading, as "1. Spec" and 1 name the first finding under the heading "1. Spec".
   - For a finding of a refuter report's run over a repair round, the bullet also gives `round <n>`, as the `diagnose` skill's "What it reads" 5 names such a finding, and the reviewer's section heading where the run holds one section per reviewer.
   - For a diagnosis record or a landing report the bullet gives no number, and a diagnosis record's heading is the record's title for its first diagnosis or the heading a later diagnosis is appended under.
   - `/roadmap` reads that report and finds the finding under that heading, in the first run or in the run of `round <n>`: by its number for a refuter report or a brief-check report, and as the whole text under the heading for a diagnosis record or a landing report.
   ```

   The sub-bullets after them ("`/roadmap` checks that the plan is open ...", the goal check, "A check that fails leaves no ruling.", "The skill says which check failed.") stay as they are. For a diagnosis record or a landing report, the finding's text that the goal check reads is the whole text under the heading.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. **The number.** A finding's number is its place, counted from 1, in the list of findings under its heading, the way the ledger already cites findings. Five real citations under this decision:
   - `agents/reviews/7-brief-check.md`, "Cases and checks" finding 4 (`plan.md`, Open item G) gives heading "4. Cases and checks", number 4.
   - `agents/reviews/12-refuter.md`, Spec 1 (the first review of step 12) gives heading "1. Spec", number 1.
   - `agents/reviews/11b-refuter.md`, Spec 1 and Standards 1 (`plan.md`, step 11b's booking) give headings "1. Spec" and "3. Standards", number 1 each.
   - `agents/reviews/6-refuter.md`, Standards 3 (`plan.md`, step 6's booking) gives heading "3. Standards", number 3.
   - `agents/reviews/11b-refuter.md`, the run over round 1, its first "3. Standards" finding gives `round 1`, heading "3. Standards", number 1, counted in that run's list (Decision 3).
2. **A diagnosis record's heading.** It is the record's title for its first diagnosis and the heading a later diagnosis is appended under (`skills/diagnose/SKILL.md`, Steps 2), and the finding's text is the whole text under it. It is not a section heading such as "Cause", since a record holding two diagnoses has two "Cause" sections. The Open item's text named the "Cause" section; the ruled bullet in the Rulings names "the heading alone", which this follows.
3. **A finding of a run over a repair round.** The bullet gives `round <n>`, the form the `diagnose` skill's "What it reads" 5 and `ordo-help` already use, and, where the round run holds one section per reviewer (as `12-refuter.md` does under the reviewer trial), that section's heading. Without it, "1. Spec" and 1 name up to three findings in `12-refuter.md`.
4. **The heading as written.** The bullet writes the heading as the report writes it, number prefix included ("1. Spec", "4. Cases and checks").

## What it must do

Item 1 changes the form of the name `/roadmap add` accepts for a finding, and nothing else. A bullet naming a finding of a refuter report's first run or of a brief-check report reads as before. A bullet naming a finding of a run over a repair round also gives `round <n>`, and the reviewer's section where the run has one per reviewer. A bullet naming a finding of a diagnosis record or a landing report gives no number, and `/roadmap` reads the whole text under its heading.

## Conventions

The rules file and the standards above. The five sub-bullets are written word for word as item 1 gives them. ASCII only; no hard wrapping.
