## Cases

Each is read on the text after the change, as the closing step would follow it.

1. A ledger whose `plan.md` Agents section holds bullets: the closing step runs the script, writes its output to `agents/reviews/closing.md`, and moves the folder on exit 0.

3. A ledger whose Agents section holds no bullet but whose `agents/agent-roles.md` holds bullets: the script is run, as in case 1.
