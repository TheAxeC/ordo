## What to build

1. **`skills/plan-orchestration/SKILL.md`, a new section `## Self-rule`**, after "The recurring-findings pass" and before "Two steps in flight". Its bullets are grouped under bold labels that are noun phrases: **Scope**, **The six kinds left open**, **Closing an open item**, **The counts**, **The choices file**, **The review of a choice**. It states:
   1. **Scope.** The section applies under `self_rule: on` in the configuration block. With `self_rule: off`, or the key absent, every open item waits for the user, as "Stops" says.
   2. **The six kinds left open**, each named in the open item by its kind:
      1. anything that reaches outside the repository or needs the user's hands or accounts, a model other than the configured one included;
      2. anything that deletes data or touches a secret;
      3. a change to the user's written rules (the rules file, the standards pages, the shared rules, `CLAUDE.md`, `.agents/plan.yaml` and the configuration block), a clash between them, or the reversal of a ruling: an option that replaces, removes or contradicts a bullet of a Rulings section, whatever its ending, or removes a step the user approved. Rewriting an approved step's text to absorb a found premise, and adding a step, are no reversal;
      4. the closing's roadmap diff, tag and pin;
      5. the user's reading of a page a step writes: the reading stays an open item and blocks no step;
      6. a choice with no clear recommendation: two options or more that the written rules, the rulings and the ADRs do not rank.
      - An option that runs `/roadmap`, `/ordo-init` or `/repo-setup` under a quoted ruling meets that skill's approval stop, since those skills take only a bullet ending "(the user)" as a quoted ruling; the item stays open for the user.
   3. **Closing an open item.** Every other open item is closed by the orchestrator the moment it is raised, whether a skill the loop invokes raised it or the loop's own "Stops" did:
      1. The open item is written in full first, as a stop is, with its options, the pros and cons of each, one recommendation and the lazy option named.
      2. The option taken is the recommended one.
      3. It is booked as the `spec` skill's "Steps / A ruling" 2 books a ruling, the option's text as the ruling's text, with one Rulings bullet whatever the ruling changes: `- Open item <L> (<date>): <the option taken, in one line, and what it unblocks> (self-rule).` That bullet is the line "Steps / A ruling" 2 names for a ruling that adds or splits a step or runs a skill.
      4. The open item moves to the Closed items as `- <date>: Open item <L>, <what was raised>: closed under self-rule, C<n>.`
      5. The choice is written to the choices file.
      6. The open item, the step's Step 0, the Rulings bullet, the Closed items, the step text the ruling rewrote and the choices file are committed by path at once, a resume point.
      7. The loop goes on with the step as the ruling leaves it: `/spec` again for a step stopped before its build, the repair round or the landing for a step stopped after it.
   4. **The counts.** An item closed under self-rule counts as a stop of its step (`spec` "Steps / A stop" 3). The stop "A step that does not converge" is never closed under self-rule, since each of its options rewrites, splits or removes a step the user approved.
   5. **The choices file**, `<ledger_root>/choices.md`, made from this skill's `templates/choices.md` at the first choice:
      - One heading per roadmap entry, `# Entry <entry number> <entry title>`, made at the entry's first choice, the entries in the order of their first choice.
      - Under it, one heading per choice, `## C<n>. <the decision, as a phrase> (<date>)`. `<n>` is one more than the file's line `Last number: C<m>`, which is then rewritten to `C<n>`, so no number is used twice.
      - Under the heading, the open item as it was raised: its options with the pros and cons of each, the recommendation with its reasons, and the lazy option.
      - Then three lines: `Taken: <the option taken>`, ``Booked: `<path>:<line>` `` (the Rulings bullet, its path relative to the repository root as it stands at the booking), and `Builds on it: <the steps, comma-separated, or none>`.
      - `Builds on it:` names the step the open item stopped and each step whose line the ruling rewrote or added. A later `/spec` whose brief rests on the choice (its step's tag names the bullet, or the brief names the bullet) adds its step to the line; the preparation commit carries the file, as the `spec` skill's Steps 6 says.
      - The file is never archived. A closed plan's choices stay in it until the user reviews them.
      - A bullet ending "(self-rule)" that a ruling of the user replaces outside a review (a `Ruled:` reply, or `/grill`) has its choice removed from the file, and the Closed items of its plan gain `- <date>: C<n>: replaced by <the ruling's name>.`
   6. **The review of a choice.** The user types, in any session on the repository:

      ```
      C<n> Agree
      C<n> => <the user's ruling, one clause per question the open item asked>
      ```

      - The session finds the choice's bullet by its name, `Open item <L>`, in the plan `Booked:` names, or, for a closed plan, in the archived `plan.md` whose folder has the same slug under `<archive_root>/`; the line number of `Booked:` is where the search starts.
      - `C<n> Agree`: the choice leaves the choices file, its entry heading with it when no choice is left under it; the bullet's ending is rewritten from "(self-rule)." to "(the user)."; that plan's Closed items gain `- <date>: C<n>, <the decision>: agreed by the user.`
      - `C<n> => <text>`: `<text>` is booked as a ruling of the user, as the `spec` skill's "Steps / A ruling" says, with the Rulings bullet `- C<n> <the decision, as a phrase> (<date>): <text>, replacing Open item <L> (the user).` The old bullet's ending is rewritten from "(self-rule)." to "(self-rule, replaced by C<n>).", which no skill reads as a ruling.
        - A step of `Builds on it:` not yet prepared, whose tag names the old bullet, has its tag rewritten to `(ruling C<n> <the decision, as a phrase>)` and its text to the new ruling.
        - When a step of `Builds on it:` has landed and the plan is open: a fix step is added before the closing, `- <k> <what changes to follow C<n>>; check: <the check> (<n> commit) (ruling C<n> <the decision, as a phrase>)`, with its own Step 0 naming the steps it corrects.
        - A step of `Builds on it:` in flight at the review gets its fix step at its landing, in the same form.
        - When such a step has landed and the plan is closed: the session drafts `/roadmap add <goal>` for the change, and the draft goes to the user at that skill's approval stop.
        - When no step of `Builds on it:` has landed or is in flight: no fix step.
        - The choice then leaves the choices file, as under `Agree`, and the Closed items gain `- <date>: C<n>, <the decision>: replaced by the user's ruling C<n>.`
      - A `C<n>` the choices file does not hold, or no choices file, is refused: the refusal names the numbers the file holds, or says there is no file, and nothing is written.
      - The files the review changed are committed by path at once, a resume point.

## Cases

This is a text step: each case is checked by reading the changed text, and the flow cases are also walked by hand on a scratch copy (verify 3).

1. `self_rule: off`, or the key absent, any open item: it waits for the user.
2. `self_rule: on`, `/refute` served a model other than the configured one: stays open, kind 1.
3. `self_rule: on`, a finding whose fix deletes a folder of the user's data: stays open, kind 2.
4. `self_rule: on`, a proposal of the recurring-findings pass to add a sentence to the rules file or a standards page: stays open, kind 3. One to change a brief's wording or a skill's step: closed.
5. `self_rule: on`, an option that removes a step the user approved, or replaces or contradicts a Rulings bullet of either ending: stays open, kind 3.
6. `self_rule: on`, the closing's `/roadmap done` diff: stays open, kind 4.
7. `self_rule: on`, a step's page waiting for the user's reading: stays open, kind 5; the next step runs.
8. `self_rule: on`, two options the written rules, rulings and ADRs do not rank: stays open, kind 6.
9. `self_rule: on`, `/spec` stops on a false premise whose recommended option rewrites the approved step's text: closed. The Rulings bullet `- Open item <L> (<date>): ...
