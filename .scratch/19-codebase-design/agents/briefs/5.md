## What to build

The ruling "repo-setup merged into ordo-init" of `plan.md` is the requirement.

1. `skills/ordo/repo-setup/templates/` moves to `skills/ordo/ordo-init/templates/`, beside `check_config.py` and its test, every file's content unchanged except where a file names its own skill or folder (`shared-rules.md`, `plan-terms.md`, `docs/glossary.md`). Files are moved with `mv`. `skills/ordo/repo-setup/` no longer exists.
2. `skills/ordo/ordo-init/SKILL.md` is one skill for three cases, chosen by what `/ordo-init` finds, never by an argument:
   - an empty folder (no tracked file, or no git repository): everything `/repo-setup` does now, with its questions, its tree, its stops and its showing of the whole tree and every file's text before writing, ending with the plan configuration;
   - an existing repository with no `plan.yaml`: what `/ordo-init` does now, and in the same draft the offer of each thing missing, which now includes the shared rules in `CLAUDE.md` (the block inserted into an existing `CLAUDE.md` as `/repo-setup sync`'s "Steps / sync" 4 drafts it, or `CLAUDE.md` written from the template when there is none) and the plan-terms block of the glossary when the repository has a glossary;
   - a repository with a `plan.yaml`: the check of the configuration as now, then the comparison of the shared-rules block and the plan-terms block with their templates through `sync_rules.py`, with every rule of "Steps / sync" 1 to 9 kept (the diff per hunk, the two directions, the quoted ruling, the errors, the two reruns, the commit).
   - The cases are tested in this order: a `plan.yaml` found is the third case; otherwise a tracked file is the second; otherwise the first.
   - The arguments both skills take now stay: the path of the repository to work on, and the folder of the `plan.yaml` with a project's name in the `projects:` form.
   - In the second case a missing glossary is offered from `templates/docs/glossary.md` like every other missing page, and a `CLAUDE.md` written from the template has its placeholders filled from what the repository shows (its name, its build and check commands), the rest asked with the questions the first case asks for them.
   - Every stop, refusal, anti-pattern and rule of both skills is kept, each once. The old refusal of `/repo-setup` on a folder with tracked files goes, since such a folder is now the second or third case.
   - The skill follows `docs/dev/skill-layout.md`: one Quick start, one "Use instead", "What it reads" and "Steps" numbered, sub-headings under Steps for the cases, every item with its "Done when" where the layout asks for it.
   - The frontmatter: `name: ordo-init`, a `description` of at most 1024 characters that covers the three cases and carries the triggers of both skills except the name `repo-setup` itself, `metadata.version: "4.0.0"`.
3. Every other file that names the skill `repo-setup`, its command `/repo-setup`, `/repo-setup sync` or its folder names `ordo-init`, `/ordo-init` or `skills/ordo/ordo-init/` instead, with the sentence around it made true:
   - the skills: `grill`, `spec`, `plan-orchestration` and its `references/self-rule.md`, `ordo-help` (its line for a new repository), `wizard`, `code-comments`, `writing`, `paper`, `grant`, and `check_config.py`'s note; a list that named both `/ordo-init` and `/repo-setup` names `/ordo-init` once; the two sequence lines of `ordo-help` and of the README become one line with the same text in both, and the two rows of `wizard`'s "Use instead" one row;
   - `docs/glossary.md` and `templates/plan-terms.md`, kept equal inside the plan-terms block; the entries for the sync, the shared-rules block and the plan-terms block say that `/ordo-init` compares them on a configured repository; every citation of a section or a numbered item of `ordo-init` or `repo-setup` in both files, and in `grill` and `gen_figures.py`, is re-pointed to the section of the merged skill that now holds it;
   - `README.md`: the row of `repo-setup` removed and the row of `ordo-init` rewritten for the three cases, "twenty-two" to "twenty-one" in both places, every list of skills, the install loop and each other mention;
   - `docs/dev/building.md`, `docs/dev/change-standard.md`, `docs/dev/scripts.md`, `docs/dev/skill-layout.md`: the paths;
   - `docs/plan.yaml` line 15: the path of the prose standard;
   - `docs/figures/gen_figures.py` and the figure it writes, `docs/figures/pipeline.svg`, regenerated by `python3 docs/figures/gen_figures.py`: the pipeline figure draws one lane `/ordo-init` with the stops of the merged skill's Stops table, and the README's alternative text and sentence on the figure follow;
   - ADRs 0001, 0002, 0014, 0026 and 0030: the folder's name only, the decision untouched; ADR 0047 is written by step 4 alone, this name included;
   - `docs/roadmap.md`: the open entries only (the lines of entries 19 and 25's goal and gate that name `repo-setup` or its paths); the lines of done and dropped entries are records and stay.
