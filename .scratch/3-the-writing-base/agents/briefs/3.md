## What to build

### 1. The review agent prompt

In `skills/writing/SKILL.md`, "Review agent prompt", the bullets are changed so that the review agent, in its one run:

- after reading the whole draft and before writing its report, checks each rule of the three pages that is judged over a section (a section's job, its tense, its four questions, a register's "What the reader looks for") against every section of the draft in turn, each rule judged on a paragraph's first sentence against every paragraph, and each rule judged over the whole draft (a count per 1000 words or per page, paragraph lengths, the shape of sections, recurring sentence shapes, runs of sentences of one length, "Objectivity / Limitations and alternative interpretations") once over the whole draft;
- applies a section rule only to a section of the kind the rule's words name, as the bullet "only where the rule's own words describe the break" already says, so a section the rule does not name gets no finding under it;
- gives a count it makes only inside the finding of a break, and writes no line for a rule it finds kept, since the prompt's bullets keep a group to its findings;
- checks each passage it reports against every other rule of the three pages, and names on its own line each other rule the passage breaks, keeping the rule that a passage stands once, in the first group in the order the prompt gives.

The bullets are worded by the builder, in the prompt's own form: short imperative bullets addressed to the agent, each one instruction, in the rules file's character set. They name kinds of rules by what the rule's words judge (a section, a paragraph's first sentence, the whole draft), never by a case draft, and they name no rule of the prose standard by its label, since the prose standard is the one the repository keeps and can differ from Ordo's. A rule of `references/rules.md` or `references/registers.md` may be named, since those pages ship with the skill. Every other bullet of the prompt keeps what it says. `metadata.version` stays "1.0.0". No other section of `SKILL.md` changes except a sentence that the change makes false, found by reading the file whole.

6. A passage has one row naming every rule it breaks, so the tables match the prompt's "stands once" rule.
7. The round's one `/refute` is the first review of this builder's diff, so it runs on the configuration's `reviewer:` model.
