   - The core principles: the most specific term; each technical term defined at first use; a pronoun ("this", "it") only with a clear antecedent; one idea per sentence; claims based on evidence, with limitations and alternative interpretations acknowledged; full forms ("do not", not "don't"); formal vocabulary.
