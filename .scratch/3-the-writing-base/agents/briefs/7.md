## What to build

Each row of step 6 not marked right by both markers, and each failure of gate conditions 1, 2 and 5 that `6-report.md` names, has a cause in the text of `/writing`: a rule the review agent was not given, a rule whose words let it act wrongly, or a rule it was given and broke. A fix is a rule the text states, never a reference to a draft, these findings or this plan. Each rule is stated once, in the one place `docs/dev/skill-layout.md`, "Where a rule goes", gives it, and follows the prose standard. Where an old sentence led to the error, the fix replaces that sentence rather than adding a second one beside it.

Rows are named as `6-marks.md` names them: manuscript rows by group and number, grant rows by number. The numbered items below are the fixes.

1. **The last pass before the hand-back.** The prompt gains one bullet, after "How you group them" and before "A draft with no break", that has the agent read its whole report once more before its final message, finding by finding and group line by group line, against the prompt's "What you report", "A finding" and "How you group them" and the section of the rules page or the registers page each finding names, and rewrite each finding or line that breaks one of their rules. The bullet names what the pass checks by the part of the prompt or the page that holds each rule, and restates none of those rules:

   - each place is in its form, with its full path, and the quoted passage stands there;

## Conventions

- The rules file's character set: ASCII, with no em or en dash and no spaced hyphen as an aside.
- One paragraph or bullet per source line, no hard wrapping.
- Plain prose: state the rule and stop; no history, no "previously", no reason longer than one clause. The prose standard's section B on semicolons and section E on sentence length apply to every new sentence and to the report.
- A rule is written once (`docs/dev/skill-layout.md`, "Where a rule goes"); a rule of the prose standard is named by its section, never restated.
