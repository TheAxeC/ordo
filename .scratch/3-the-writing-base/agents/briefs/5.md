## What it must do

As "What to build" says, kind by kind. The prompt stays one block the session gives whole. Each new sentence in it is a rule the agent can follow by reading. No sentence names a draft, a project, a person, a finding number or this plan, since the skill's "Rules" say everything specific to a repository comes from `.agents/plan.yaml`. The examples a rule gives are general, such as "as in finding N" for a reference to another finding.

## Conventions

- The rules file's character set: ASCII, with no em or en dash and no spaced hyphen as an aside.
- One paragraph or bullet per source line, no hard wrapping.
- Plain prose: state the rule and stop; no history, no "previously", no reason longer than one clause.
- A rule is written once (`docs/dev/skill-layout.md`, "Where a rule goes"); a rule of the prose standard is named by its section, never restated.
