- A case of a code step (a script, or a product's code) becomes a test of the step only when the rules file's test rule calls for a test of it.

- No prototype script stands in for such a test.

## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. `docx_paragraphs.py` reads footnotes and endnotes after the body, numbered on from it, leaves out headers and footers, and numbers only the paragraphs it prints. D16 says "the text of each paragraph"; a grant's footnotes hold prose the review must read, while its header and footer hold form text and page numbers the author does not write; a number for an empty paragraph would give the agent a place with nothing to quote. Five real cases, each quoted by the builder with its output line or its absence: a body paragraph and a table cell of the NWO XS grant, its page-break paragraph (absent), its paragraph with a tab stop, and a footnote of the HexC proposal.
2. A passage that breaks two rules stands in the first group in the order prose standard, `rules.md`, `registers.md`. D29 sets groups by rule and document order inside a group, and says nothing on which group a passage of two rules goes to; a fixed order gives one answer the case files of step 2 can state.
3. The prose standard's rule against describing what the page is about to do holds over the source's exception for roadmap sentences in an Introduction, which is not carried, since ADR 0002 says no rule that contradicts the prose standard is carried.
