# Step 3, the cases ruling (round 0)

The builder's first run of the brief's "Cases" found places where the brief's dictated text would contradict the prose standard (case 5), restate it (case 4) or leave a source rule without a row (case 3). The brief `agents/briefs/3.md` still governs; these rulings replace the parts they name.

1. **"a large number of" in the wordy table.** (a): its replacement is "the number itself" with the prose standard named ("section A: a vague qualifier is replaced with the number"), since "many" is on section A's vague list. The record says the source's "many" was replaced and why.
2. **Synonym cycling.** (a): the row carries the pattern, the source's example (students, learners, participants, subjects) and why it fails; its fix cell names "prose standard, section D". The record says "per section" is not carried because section D's rule, one term per concept on the whole page, is stricter.
3. **Fix cells of the section D rows.** (a): each fix cell of forced groups of three, uniform paragraph length and mirror structure names "prose standard, section D" and carries only what D does not state (the source's examples, such as "Methods can be procedural, Discussion can be exploratory"). The source's example "a 2-sentence paragraph after a 10-sentence paragraph" is not carried, since a 10-sentence paragraph breaks section D's "under roughly four sentences"; the record says so.
4. **Source rules the brief left without an instruction.**
   - (i) "Use third person unless discipline conventions allow first person" is carried in the core principles, beside the Engineering and CS register's voice.
   - (ii) "One idea per sentence (or clearly connected ideas)" is carried with its qualifier.
   - (iii) "Prefer short sentences for complex ideas" is not carried as its own rule: section E's limit (under roughly 20 words unless the mechanism needs more) is the rule kept, and the page names section E. The record says so.
   - (iv) The "why it's flagged" and "better alternatives" columns of the flagged-term table are not carried: section A asks for the most precise word, and several of the alternatives are themselves on section A's lists. The record says so.
   - (v) The fixes for a run of equal-length sentences (a sentence of 10 words or fewer inserted, two short sentences combined, the paragraph read aloud) are carried in the fix cell of the sentence-length row.
   - (vi) The clarity test carries the source's rule as written, load-bearing paragraphs get several drafts and supporting paragraphs get one, and its reason: a text that gives every paragraph equal weight fails the test.
   - (vii) The source lines naming which agents or drafting step use a file are not rules; the record marks them "not a rule: the page's opening paragraph names its readers".
5. **Passive voice in the Engineering and CS register.** Carried as the brief says. The page states it beside section E's rule: in a method step the actor is usually irrelevant, which is the case section E allows. Case 5's greps list both lines.
