# Deviations of `tutor` from the source skill `teach`

The source is mattpocock's skill `teach`, whose files are saved in `sources/teach/` of this ledger with the source repository's page on the skill as `docs-teach.md`. Brief 5, "What is on the tree", says that two characters of the saved files are written in ASCII: the range "1-10" in `GLOSSARY-FORMAT.md` and "{...}" in `MISSION-FORMAT.md`; the source as read writes them, as the report of step 5 says, with a range character and an ellipsis character outside ASCII. That statement rests on the brief and the report of step 5 alone and cannot be checked from the saved files, which hold only the ASCII forms; apart from those two characters, the saved files are taken to be the source as read. A character outside ASCII is written here by its name in angle brackets.

The differences that the layout, ASCII and the prose standard force, listed by kind:
- **Sections.** The source's sections became the layout's nine sections: the title and paragraph, Quick start, Use instead with the row `/wait-what`, What it reads, Steps with "The call", "Teaching", "Explaining" and "The page", the reference sections "The teaching workspace" and "Knowledge, skills and wisdom", Stops, Anti-patterns and Rules. Each part of the source stands in `tutor` here:
  - frontmatter `name`, `description`, `disable-model-invocation`, `argument-hint`: the rows of the table above.
  - opening paragraph (a stateful request over several sessions): the paragraph under `# Tutor`.
  - "Teaching Workspace": "What it reads" item 2 (the folder, and where its paths resolve); "The teaching workspace" (each file).
  - "Philosophy": "Knowledge, skills and wisdom" (the three things, the topics that need more skills or more knowledge); "Teaching" item 4 (resources while `RESOURCES.md` is not well populated); Rules 2 (parametric knowledge).
  - "Fluency vs Storage Strength": "Knowledge, skills and wisdom" (the two strengths); "Teaching" item 6 (desirable difficulty and its three techniques).
  - "Lessons": "Teaching" item 8, "The lesson" (the file, its name and its form) and item 9 (the lesson opened); "The teaching workspace", `lessons/*.html`.
  - "Assets": "Teaching" item 7; "The teaching workspace", `assets/*`; Anti-patterns row 3.
  - "The Mission": "Teaching" items 2 and 3; Stops rows "The mission interview" and "The confirmation before the mission is changed"; Rules 1; Anti-patterns row 2.
  - "Zone Of Proximal Development": "Teaching" item 5; "Teaching" item 8 (the lesson in the zone).
  - "Knowledge": "Teaching" item 6 (only what the skill requires, knowledge first, from `RESOURCES.md`, difficulty the enemy); "Teaching" item 4 (`RESOURCES.md` keeps track of the resources); "Teaching" item 8 (citations); "Knowledge, skills and wisdom", Knowledge.
  - "Skills": "Teaching" item 6 (difficulty the tool, interactive lessons, the feedback loop, the quiz rules); "Knowledge, skills and wisdom", Skills; Anti-patterns row 4.
  - "Acquiring Wisdom": Rules 5 and 6; "Knowledge, skills and wisdom", Wisdom.
  - "Reference Documents": "Teaching" item 8, "The reference documents"; "The teaching workspace", `reference/*.html` and `reference/GLOSSARY.md`.
  - "`NOTES.md`": Rules 4; "The teaching workspace", `NOTES.md`.
  - `MISSION-FORMAT.md`: `references/mission-format.md`, named from "What it reads" item 3, "Teaching" item 2, the Stops row "The mission interview" and "The teaching workspace".
  - `RESOURCES-FORMAT.md`: `references/resources-format.md`, named from "What it reads" item 3, "Teaching" item 4 and "The teaching workspace".
  - `LEARNING-RECORD-FORMAT.md`: `references/learning-record-format.md`, named from "What it reads" item 3, "Explaining" item 5, Rules 3 and "The teaching workspace".
  - `GLOSSARY-FORMAT.md`: `references/glossary-format.md`, named from "What it reads" item 3, "Teaching" item 8 and "The teaching workspace".
  - `agents/openai.yaml`: not shipped.
- **Anti-patterns.** The "Anti-pattern" cells are made from the source's prohibitions ("Never trust your parametric knowledge", "never inline code a future lesson would duplicate", "Don't give the user any clues", a lesson before the mission), with one row for the explain mode. The "Why it fails" and "Do instead" cells, which the layout's table asks for in each row, are new text with no counterpart in the source's `SKILL.md`, such as "It can teach what is made up, and the user has nothing to check it against" and "The lessons drift apart and stop looking like one course"; the first draws on the source's page `docs-teach.md`, "Common questions".
- **Second person.** The source's "you" (the agent) and "your first job" are written as instructions.
- **ASCII and the prose standard.**
  - The spaced-hyphen asides of the source are reworded, among them "stateful request - they", "from the lessons - cheat sheets", "the mission - the reason", "This is normal - make sure", "citations - links", "immediately - and ideally automatically", "real-world interaction - testing", "attempt to answer - but", "revisited later - reference documents will be" and "these documents - they are useful".
  - "Think Tufte." became "in the manner of Edward Tufte".
  - "completable very quickly" became "fast to complete".
  - "Reuse is the default, not the exception" became "Reuse of components is the default".
  - "real-world goals" became "real-world aims" and "the real goal" became "the real aim", since **goal** is a glossary term.
  - "designed for quick reference" became "made for reference at a glance".
  - "steps to take (for instance, yoga poses)" became "real-world actions, such as yoga poses", since **step** is a plan term.
- **Format pages.** `cmp` of the saved glossary, mission and learning-record pages against their copies prints nothing, and `diff` of the saved `RESOURCES-FORMAT.md` against `resources-format.md` prints one line, the link path. The two characters of the ASCII rows are already ASCII in the saved pages, so the copies carry the saved pages' characters unchanged; that the source as read differs in those two characters rests on brief 5 and the report of step 5 and cannot be checked from the saved files.

`wait-what`: moved with `mv`. The `diff` case prints no line, so its `SKILL.md` is unchanged.

The format pages name `GLOSSARY.md`, and the workspace writes that file as `reference/GLOSSARY.md` (ruling D).
