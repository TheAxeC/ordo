## What to build

1. The README layout. `references/project_layout.md`, "The README", states the layout of ADR 0057 in this order, each heading as written here:
   - `# <project>`, followed by one line: what the project is, its status and its venue.
   - `## In short`.
   - `## What it is and why`, with `### The problem`, `### Why it matters` (for a method applied to a clinical dataset, its clinical relevance) and `### What it does`.
   - `## Research questions and claims`.
   - `## Data`, one `### <dataset>` per dataset with its source, version, access, local path and cluster path.
   - `## Method`, with `### The approach` and `### What is not claimed`.
   - `## Constraints and limits`: what counts as good enough, the limits on storage, compute, time and access, and the setting with its count.
   - `## Related work`, with `### The closest prior work`, linking the novelty pages, and `### What this project adds`.
   - `## Results and how to reproduce`, one `### <result>` per result with its number, its file under `code/results/`, its command, and whether it beats the baseline and reaches what was set as good enough.
   - `## Papers`.
   - `## Dead ends (do not retry)`, one `### <method>` per dead end with its folder in `code/attic/` and why it failed.
   - `## Next`: a short numbered list, from which a finished item moves to Results or Dead ends.
   - `## Working state`, with `### The stage and campaign that stand`, `### Where things live` and `### Cluster facts`.
   - Once a paper is the canonical record, Method and Related work hold a pointer to it and what the paper does not hold.
   - A section past 200 lines moves to `notes/<slug>.md` and the README keeps the heading with a link to it; `<slug>` is the section's heading in lower case with each run of characters other than letters and digits a single `-` and no `-` at either end (`## Dead ends (do not retry)` gives `dead-ends-do-not-retry`); a `notes/<slug>.md` that exists is never overwritten, and the next free name `notes/<slug>-2.md`, `-3` and so on is taken, as `idea`'s progress files are named. An empty section reads `None yet.`
   - What each old section held goes to its new place: the dataset and the reason it was chosen, and where its raw inputs stand, go to `## Data`; everything else of the old nine keeps its content under its new heading.
