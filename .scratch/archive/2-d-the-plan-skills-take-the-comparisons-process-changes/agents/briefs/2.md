## What to build

1. `skills/refute/SKILL.md`, "Rules": a bullet that the reviewer invokes no skill and starts no agent; it reads, runs the commands the skill names, and writes its report.
2. `skills/refute/SKILL.md`, a new reference section "The verdicts", after "The four headings", and Steps 5 and 6 pointing at it:
   - One verdict per item of the brief's "What to build", in the brief's numbering: holds (the diff does what the item's text says, the evidence named), violated (it does not; the finding under its heading is named), not applicable (the item does not apply to this tree, with the reason).
   - One verdict per case of the brief's "Cases": met (the test or the reading gives the expected result), partial (part of the expected result, the missing part named), unmet (the expected result is not given), not verifiable (neither a test nor a reading can settle it here, with what would).
   - A verdict of violated, partial or unmet always has a finding under one of the four headings; a finding and a verdict name each other.
   - Over a repair round, the verdicts are given again for the whole diff since the base.
3. `skills/refute/SKILL.md`, "The four headings": each finding carries its failure scenario: the concrete input or state and the wrong result it gives, or, for a finding in text, the reader and what the text leads them to do wrong.
4. `skills/refute/SKILL.md`: the heading "Declined to judge" replaces "Not checked" wherever the skill names it (Steps 6 and the Anti-patterns row "An unchecked point"): each point the reviewer did not check, or declined because it is the user's call or outside what a read and a rerun can settle, with the reason.
5. `skills/refute/templates/report.md`: a section "Verdicts" after "Verification", with the two lists of item 2; each finding line of the four headings and of "Repair round <n>, refuted" gains `failure scenario: <...>`; "Not checked" becomes "Declined to judge"; the repair round section gains its verdicts.
6. The description of `skills/refute/SKILL.md` names the verdicts beside the four headings, at most 1,024 characters by the gate's command; `metadata.version` goes to "1.7.0".
7. `skills/plan-retro/SKILL.md`, "Grouping", fourth bullet: the lists it does not read are the Verification, Verdicts, Declined to judge, Not checked, Closed, Closures and Usage lists, and fenced lines. "Not checked" stays in the list because the archived reports the skill also reads carry it; the bullet states no history. `metadata.version` of `plan-retro` goes up one patch.
