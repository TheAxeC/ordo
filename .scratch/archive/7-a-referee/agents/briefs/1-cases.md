# Step 1, the ruling on the cases handed back before the build (round 0)

The builder's first read found two places where the brief's rules give a wrong result and one point the brief leaves unsaid. Each is ruled here, inside the step's part. The brief stands in everything else, item 7 on `skills/academic/paper-review-panel/SKILL.md` included.

1. The glossary entry **round, of an interview**. It stands inside the plan-terms block of `docs/glossary.md` (`grep -n 'ordo:plan-terms' docs/glossary.md` prints lines 5 and 140, and the entry is on line 107), which is the copy of `skills/ordo/repo-setup/templates/plan-terms.md`. Ruling: that entry is not changed, in either file. A plan term is copied into every repository that uses the plan skills, and a referee review's round is no plan term. Only the new entry, **round, of a referee review**, is written, outside the block, where the page's order puts it among the entries outside the block. It says what the first round and the second round of a referee review are, and that the bare "round" in `referee` and in text that describes `referee` means this. The part of "The places that list a skill" 5 that asks the entry **round, of an interview** to gain words is withdrawn. No path is added.
2. A plain request that does not say the round. Ruling: the round is settled when the call is read, in item 1, before the run folder is made. A call `/referee re-review ...` is a second round, and every other call `/referee ...` is a first round. A plain request that says it is a revision, a second round or a re-review is a second round. A plain request that does not say the round is asked once, which round it is, as a stop with its own row in the Stops table, and the run goes on under the subsection of the answer. The intake no longer asks the round: the bullet "the round, only for a plain request that does not say it" is withdrawn from the intake.
3. The EIC in a second round with all five reviewers. Ruling: the five reviewers of second round 4 are fresh agents under the full contract, the EIC among them, beside the EIC that made the check of the revision in second round 3, since the contract's first call is made without the paper. The selection reads the EIC's check and the five reports.

The report carries this ruling under the cases' first run.
