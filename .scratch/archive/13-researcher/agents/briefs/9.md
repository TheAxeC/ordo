## What to build

Each page of the skill that says when a job of a piece of code is submitted names, on a cluster, the builder's small local test as the one job of the piece submitted before the reviewer reads the code, with the reason in one clause: nothing may compute on the login node, and the test produces no result. Every other job still waits for a reviewer's report with no finding that makes a result false.

- `skills/academic/researcher/agents/research_builder_agent.md` line 10: the sentence on what the builder never submits names the test job as the one job it submits, on a cluster, before the review.
- `skills/academic/researcher/agents/research_reviewer_agent.md` line 10: "before any job of it is submitted" is made true: the review comes before every job of the piece other than the builder's small local test.
- `skills/academic/researcher/references/slurm.md` "A small local test": one bullet saying that the builder's test job is the one job of a piece submitted before its review, with the reason.
- `skills/academic/researcher/SKILL.md` "research" item 8 line 294 and the Anti-patterns row at line 692: each names the builder's small local test as the one exception.
- No other line changes. No new term enters the glossary. The version stays.
