## What it must do

Each page is copied and edited as "What to build" says. A page's own prose is the source's: the prose standard's rules on sentence length, lists and vocabulary are not applied to the copied text, since a copy keeps its source's words. The prose standard governs only the words an edit writes. The same holds for the glossary: a copied sentence keeps the source's sense of a word such as "reviewer", "finding", "round" or "step", and the rule of `docs/dev/skill-layout.md` on glossary terms holds only for the words an edit writes.
