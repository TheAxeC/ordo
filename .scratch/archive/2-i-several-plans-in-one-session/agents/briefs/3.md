## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The entry is written at the end of the preflight, after the authority check and the verify-list compare, so a refused `/spec` writes no entry.
2. Of two steps in `/spec` together, the later to reach its Steps 5 makes the compare, since the earlier has no brief of the other to read.
3. `<the closing's next part>` names one of the three parts of the closing line of `skills/plan/templates/plan.md`, so the position line uses the closing's own words.
4. Another plan's record that the session wrote is fixed in place at landing, since the session would fix it at that plan's next resume point anyway, and the landing's red line has no other cause. A record the session did not write goes to the user, as `spec` Steps 1 and `plan-orchestration` "Resuming" already leave such a change alone.
5. The rule for a waiting `/spec` sits in `spec` "Steps / The brief check", where the wait happens, and `plan-orchestration` points at it.
6. The brief-check agent's id goes into the `preparing` entry's existing key `brief_check`, as `running: <agent id>`, rather than a new key, so ADR 0006's "the brief check in `brief_check`" holds from the agent's start.
7. A step at `landing: preparing` under "The review of a choice" counts as not yet prepared, since no brief of it has been committed for a builder.
