## Decisions taken in this brief (each reversible, none silent)

1. The names `plan_cost.py`, `prices.txt` and `plan_cost.test.sh`, and a table of space-separated columns with `#` comment lines, which a person reads and edits in any editor and the script parses without a library. Tabs are out, since the verify list's ASCII check flags a tab in any tracked file.
2. The last entry of a duplicated pair carries the response's counts: on this machine's 12373 duplicated pairs the last entry always has the largest output count, and the earlier ones are partial counts of the same response.
