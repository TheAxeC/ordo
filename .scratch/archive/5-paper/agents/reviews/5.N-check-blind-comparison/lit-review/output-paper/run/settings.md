For each method, the work that measures how it fails (uniqueness and degeneracy of PARAFAC2, the alignment assumption of tensor regression on voxel grids) and the methods that stand in its place for the same question. Target: 12 to 22 included sources. Sub-topics and minimums: multiway and tensor PLS (3); tensor regression (3); decompositions with an unaligned mode (4); failure analyses and supervised or coupled variants of those decompositions (2).

For each method, the work that measures how it fails (entropic bias, non-convexity and local minima of GW, cost and scaling) and the methods that stand in its place. Neighbouring areas: tensor methods (Theme 1), set encoders (Theme 3), registration pipelines (Theme 4); a source of those areas is included only when central to this scope and flagged as an overlap.
