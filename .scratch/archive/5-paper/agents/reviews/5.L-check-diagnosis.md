## Cause

The merge run's prompt, as `skills/paper/SKILL.md` "The prompts of a literature search" and Steps 8 build it, gives the merge run no search but the failure handling of a failed gate, and gives the session no place to hand it the open work of the theme runs. The theme runs stop at their target and pass on leads, sources cut to hold the target, unconfirmed works and open citation loops, as academic-paper's theme runs do; academic-paper's merges assess that work and search further, and `paper`'s merge lists it as "not assessed". The probe that shows it is the read of probe 1 and 2: the merge transcript's only search is the currency search, beside the 40 and 30 additions academic-paper's merges took from exactly that open work. Not verified by a run, by the user's ruling.

## Operation and other inputs

The operation that goes wrong: the merge-run rules, `SKILL.md:879` ("Where a gate fails, the merge run applies the failure handling strategies of its page itself ...
