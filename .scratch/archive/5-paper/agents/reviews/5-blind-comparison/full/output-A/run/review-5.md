### Strengths
1. The survival design is now stated as it was carried out. 5.2: "Each patient's risk in the survival design came from the fold in which that patient was held out. For the fused model and ECG-only BTTN, it was the mean log-risk of three networks trained on the other four folds. The clinical Cox model was fitted on the same four folds. These cross-validated risks were read at every horizon without refitting." This agrees with the analysis: five folds stratified by event status, a three-network ensemble per test fold, and a penalised Cox model fitted on the same training folds.
2. The Conclusion's design implication now rests on a result. 7: "the results suggest that a clinical term kept outside the block-term factors adds to the discrimination of the ECG term. At one year, the fused model reached 0.7776 [0.7664, 0.7888] and the ECG term alone 0.7397 [0.7275, 0.7517]. These two intervals do not overlap, although the study reports no paired interval for this difference." The values equal the saved one-year results, and the qualifier on the missing paired interval keeps the claim at the strength the intervals carry.
3. The Discussion separates the size of the gain from its uncertainty. 6.1: "The gain of the fused model over the clinical model is small and consistent in direction. The one-year intervals overlap, and the study reports no interval of the difference that would establish it."
4. The label source is argued and its limits are quantified. 4.1: "Hospital diagnoses were preferred to labels read from the ECG, since only 65.4\% of 700 AF-labelled ECGs in MIMIC-IV showed AF on expert review"; 6.3 gives the pooled PPV of 88\% and sensitivity of 80\% of administrative AF definitions and the 73.7\% capture of a discharge diagnosis.
5. The occlusion readout reports the raw and the energy-normalised profile side by side and says why they differ. 5.4: "The QRS complex has the highest amplitude of the beat, so zeroing it removes the most energy and changes the spectrogram most. Per unit of energy removed, the profile was highest in the PR window, at 0.00216."
