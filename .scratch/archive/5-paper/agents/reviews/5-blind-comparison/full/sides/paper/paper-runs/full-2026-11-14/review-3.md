### Strengths
1. The occlusion readout can now be reproduced from the text alone. 4.3: "a window of 12 samples (24 ms at 500 Hz) was set to zero in every beat of one lead. The window ran from 6 samples before to 5 samples after a fixed offset from each R peak. Each offset was rounded to a whole sample towards the R peak." Table 5 gives the centres and the spans that the occluded samples cover, and both agree with the readout as carried out (for example the PR window, centres $-125$ to $-75$ ms, spans $-136$ to $-62$ ms).
2. The offsets outside the five windows are now accounted for, including their effect on the normalisation. 4.3: "They are shown in Figure 3, and their removed energy counts in the mean energy over all leads and offsets that the normalisation adds. They enter no window value."
3. The claim about the fused model's factors is held to what the paper shows. 6.2: "The fused model is trained as one network, so its factors are fitted beside the clinical term and may differ from those of ECG-only BTTN", and 6.2 ends on the limit of the separation: "It does not close it for information that the ECG itself carries, which the factors may encode in ways a reader does not expect."
4. The comparison with the clinical model is reported with its uncertainty at the strength the intervals carry. 5.2: "It overlapped the interval of the clinical model, 0.7597 [0.7488, 0.7713], and the study reports no paired interval for this difference. The two estimates are therefore reported side by side."
5. The PPV limitation now names the right group. 6.3: "This proportion exceeds the one-year rate of the survival cohort, 1,855 in 50,545, whose one-year negatives include the patients with onset after one year. The PPVs of Table 4 are therefore upper values for that population."
6. The outcome definition is justified by measured label quality. 4.1: "Hospital diagnoses were preferred to labels read from the ECG, since only 65.4% of 700 AF-labelled ECGs in MIMIC-IV showed AF on expert review", with the incompleteness of coded diagnoses quantified in 6.3 from three validation sources.
