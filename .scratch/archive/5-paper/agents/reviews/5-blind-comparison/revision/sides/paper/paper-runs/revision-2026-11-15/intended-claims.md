# Intended claims of the revision

Claims that the text added or changed by this revision makes, written before the first patch, from the items of `items.md`, the sources of `review.md` and the `.bib`, and the project's code and saved results.

1. The ten seeds vary only the random initialisation of k-means, spectral clustering and the Gaussian mixture; the cohort, the features, the two foundation-model embeddings and the trained autoencoder are fixed across seeds, and Ward clustering and HDBSCAN are deterministic and fitted once, so the seed spread measures the stability of the clustering step on a fixed cohort. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
2. The standard deviation over seeds of the centralized k-means ARI is 0.000 for raw flattening, the hand-crafted features and HuBERT-ECG and 0.001 for ECGFounder. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
3. The saved 95% half-width over seeds is 0.008 for raw flattening with the Gaussian mixture and below 0.001 for the other seeded raw-flattening and hand-crafted entries. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
4. With ten paired seeds the smallest attainable two-sided Wilcoxon p-value is 0.002, so a p-value at this floor fixes the direction of a contrast and not its size; the differences in mean ARI (0.242, 0.470, 0.174) are the effect sizes of the tested contrasts. | evidence: definitional | sources: the project's results
5. The hand-crafted k-means partition has a purity of 0.901, against 0.506 for raw flattening and a majority-class proportion of 0.504. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
6. HDBSCAN is given no cluster count, labels low-density points as noise, uses a minimum cluster size of the larger of 10 and one fiftieth of the records, and keeps noise points as their own label, which counts as one additional cluster for ARI, AMI, NMI and the Fowlkes-Mallows index; purity counts noise as misassigned, the silhouette excludes noise, and the largest-cluster fraction counts non-noise clusters over all records. | evidence: definitional | sources: hdbscan2013, the project's results
7. HDBSCAN labels every record as noise for raw flattening, the conv-AE and HuBERT-ECG, and for the hand-crafted features and ECGFounder its larger cluster holds 0.140 and 0.202 of the records. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
8. The ARI, AMI, NMI, V-measure, Fowlkes-Mallows index, purity, silhouette, largest-cluster fraction and supervised ceiling have the formulas, ranges and chance or one-cluster values given in the metrics table; NMI with the arithmetic-mean normalisation equals the V-measure. | evidence: definitional | sources: hubertarabie1985, vinh2010, Danon_2005, rosenberg2007vmeasure, Fowlkes_1983, rousseeuw1987, the project's results
9. The PTB-XL records are 16-bit WFDB samples at 1 microvolt per least significant bit, released at 100 Hz as well as 500 Hz; the study read the 100 Hz version of release 1.0.3 into 32-bit floating-point arrays of the physical signal, 1000 samples by 12 leads, with no further conversion before the representation steps; MIT-BIH beats were read the same way from the two-lead 360 Hz records. | evidence: empirical | sources: ptbxl2020, the project's results
10. AF is defined on the ECG by irregular RR intervals and the absence of discernible repeating P waves, and a single-lead tracing of at least 30 s showing these signs is diagnostic. | evidence: normative | sources: Van_Gelder_2024
11. Rhythm classes including AF have been classified from single-lead recordings. | evidence: empirical | sources: Hannun_2019, clifford2017af
12. Only the hand-crafted features use a single lead (lead II); raw flattening, the conv-AE and both foundation models take all 12 leads; the single-lead set leaves out inter-lead information and can be computed from a single-lead recording. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
13. Each representation has the input, transformation, output dimension and pretraining stated in the representation table: raw flattening of about 2000 values, a 32-value conv-AE code, 16 hand-crafted features (all named), 768 values from HuBERT-ECG on the first 5 s at 100 Hz, and 1024 values from ECGFounder on the 10 s strip resampled to 500 Hz; on MIT-BIH a raw beat window of about 400 values, 21 hand-crafted beat features and a 32-value conv-AE code. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results, hubertecg2024, ecgfounder2024
14. HuBERT-ECG was pretrained by self-supervision on 9.1 million 12-lead ECGs with 5 s inputs at 100 Hz, band-pass filtering to 0.05 to 47 Hz and rescaling to [-1, 1], and its base configuration has 93 million parameters. | evidence: empirical | sources: hubertecg2024
15. ECGFounder was pretrained with supervision on 10,771,552 ECGs with 150 label categories, including rhythm descriptions, takes a 12 x 5000 input at 500 Hz, applies filtering and per-segment z-scoring in pretraining, and has 76.3 million parameters. | evidence: empirical | sources: ecgfounder2024, arxiv_2410_04133
16. The study's foundation-model inputs were not filtered as their pretraining data were. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results, hubertecg2024, arxiv_2410_04133
17. HuBERT-ECG and ECGFounder share public weights and 12-lead input, represent a self-supervised transformer and a supervised convolutional network, and are both evaluated in the closest benchmark, which reports ECGFounder's embedding as aligned with the clinical label and HuBERT-ECG's as organised by dataset; ECGFounder was included for that report. | evidence: empirical | sources: lookingbeyond2026, the project's results
18. The closest benchmark evaluates ECG-FM, ECGFounder, three sizes of HuBERT-ECG and ECG-JEPA as frozen extractors on four 12-lead datasets including PTB-XL, with probes under class-balanced 15-fold cross-validation and a representation-wise evaluation that by its own statement earlier benchmarks lack, including the ARI of Gaussian-mixture clusters; it includes no hand-crafted baseline and not MIT-BIH. | evidence: empirical | sources: lookingbeyond2026
19. BenchECG, Al-Masud et al. and Xu et al. benchmark ECG foundation models on supervised downstream tasks, Berger et al. find that a randomly initialised encoder with linear evaluation matches pretraining on many tasks, and a review names the absence of standardised evaluation as a challenge; none of BenchECG, Al-Masud et al. and Berger et al. evaluates clustering of embeddings or includes a hand-crafted baseline. | evidence: empirical | sources: Lunelli_2026, arxiv_2509_25095, Xu_2025, arxiv_2602_17531, Han_2026
20. The contribution of the study is an evaluation protocol for label-free ECG structure: chance-corrected, patient-grouped clustering scores read against a supervised ceiling on the same representation and folds and against cluster sizes, a conditioner sweep, a hand-crafted baseline beside two foundation models on PTB-XL and MIT-BIH, and two deployment stress tests; no new algorithm is proposed. | evidence: theoretical | sources: the project's results, lookingbeyond2026, Lunelli_2026, arxiv_2509_25095, arxiv_2602_17531
21. Other ECG foundation models with public weights include ST-MEM, ECG-JEPA, MERL, ECGFM-KED, ECG-FM and ECG-CPC, and they differ in training objective. | evidence: empirical | sources: arxiv_2509_25095, Lunelli_2026, arxiv_2402_09450, Kim_2027, arxiv_2403_06659, Tian_2024, McKeen_2025
22. Every foundation-model statement holds for HuBERT-ECG and ECGFounder, and a model trained with another objective could rank differently. | evidence: theoretical | sources: the project's results
23. ECGFounder's high clusterability is consistent with its supervised pretraining on diagnostic labels; the hand-crafted features' clusterability follows from measuring the defining criteria of AF; HuBERT-ECG holds the label (ceiling 0.899) along directions of small variance that clustering does not follow, consistent with a dataset-organised embedding; raw flattening and the conv-AE score as a majority-class assignment because neither places the rhythm difference along dominant axes. 26. The federated simulation assigns patients to ten sites at random or by per-class Dirichlet proportions with concentration 0.3, drops sites with fewer than two records, runs 15 rounds of three local Lloyd iterations with count-weighted centroid averaging, draws a new partition and initialisation per seed, and fits the power transform and draws the initial centroids on the pooled records. | evidence: empirical | sources: fedavg2017, the project's results

The conclusions hold for two binary tasks, five clustering algorithms, two named foundation models and a simulated federation, and make no claim about multi-class rhythm structure, where RR irregularity alone would not single out AF. | evidence: theoretical | sources: Zheng_2020, Perez_Alday_2020, ptbxl2020, clifford2017af
