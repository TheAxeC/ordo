## Correction to the brief

The brief states that the raw-flatten GMM seed SD is 0.0082, read from "`code/results/summary.csv` row 5, ARI std column 0.008227". That column is `_ci95` (header lines 1-2 of `summary.csv`), written by `_ci95` in `code/experiments/benchmark.py` lines 112-114 as `1.96 * x.std(ddof=1) / np.sqrt(len(x))`. The SD is therefore 0.008227327 x 3.1623 / 1.96 = 0.0260172774 / 1.96 = 0.0133, as `code-facts.md` section 13 item 3 says. Direct check from the ten values in `code/results/results_raw.csv` rows 23-32: five values 0.0251881 and five within 0.0000138 of 0, mean 0.0125952; each deviation is about +/-0.0125945; sum of squares about 10 x 0.0125945^2 = 10 x 0.00015862 = 0.0015862; / 9 = 0.00017625; sqrt = 0.01328. The manuscript uses 0.0133.

Exact p-value. Under the sign-flip null each of the 2^10 = 1024 sign assignments to the ten absolute differences has probability 1/1024. With midranks for ties, the ranks still sum to 1 + 2 + ... + 10 = 55 and every rank is at least 1. With all ten differences positive, T+ = 55, the maximum; every other assignment makes at least one difference negative and lowers T+ by that difference's rank, so exactly one assignment gives T+ = 55 and exactly one (all negative) gives T+ = 0. The two-sided p-value is P(|T+ - 27.5| >= 27.5) = 2/1024 = 1/512 = 0.001953125, reported as 0.002. Any assignment has its mirror image at the same distance from 27.5, so no outcome gives a two-sided p-value below 2/1024; 1/512 is therefore the smallest value the exact test can give with ten pairs and no zero differences. Code-facts item 5 and the arithmetic above give all-positive differences in all four contrasts, so the value holds for each contrast.
