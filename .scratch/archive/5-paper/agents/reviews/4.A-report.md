### Deviations from academic-paper 3.2.0, as the skill now stands

Each place `paper` differs from academic-paper 3.2.0 for plan and format-convert, with the ruling, the ADR or the decision that makes it. A difference with none is not built.

1. The intake of plan mode is the `config.md` interview of the topic, the materials in hand (including ideas) and the structure preference, in place of the 3-question simplified interview: D24 (the `config.md` interview, as the tag in the figures names it) and ruling 14.
2. The mentor is one agent per run, resumed with each answer, and writes `checkpoint.md` after each round: ADR 0027 and decision 15.
3. The checkpoint carries four fields academic-paper's checkpoint lacks, the rounds in all, the step, the commitment protocol on or off and the skipped chapters, because a fresh mentor that continues a run needs each to apply the limits and the protocol: ruling 19 of round 2, by decision 15.
4. The mentor gives no praise and no encouragement formula: ADR 0002, round-1 ruling 2. The reflection stays a likely moment for an insight, as in the source: ruling 2.
5. The wording advisory opens with the label "Wording-pattern advisory" in plain words in place of the source's tag: ADR 0002, ruling 18.
6. The structure architect and the argument builder are one planner, and the stress test is the planner's rating of each answer, started fresh for each: decisions 6 and 16 (D6, D16).
7. Step 0 refers a missing foundation to `/paper lit-review` and `idea`, not to deep-research: decision 5 (D5).
8. The offers after 8 rounds on a chapter and 30 in all: the source names 30 rounds in one list and 15 in another, and `paper` takes 30 (the note of `plan-detailed.svg`); Step 1 has no cap of its own, as the source: ruling 15.
9. The plan's handoff: full mode only when every condition of the gate holds (decision 3, D3), the mentor's message with its cross-chapter observation beside the chapter plan (ruling 27), `next: pause` and `next: outline-only` written into `paused.md` or `final.md` (ruling 28), and a plan run's files copied into a full run with `cp` and `cmp` (ruling 30).
10. Chicago is asked in its two forms at intake, and the last chapter summary is confirmed after the outline is written: round-1 rulings 3 and 4.
11. The formatter writes two patches, the second applied only to the conversion copy, and the session compiles the copy and each drawing with `latexmk` and `pdftocairo` before `pandoc` runs; for a run that changes the citation style a second formatter start writes the conversion patch after the copy is compiled in the new style: round-1 rulings 23 and 24, ruling 9 of round 2 (ruling BA), ADR 0027.
12. A numbered style is printed into the copy as numbers and a list from the compile's `.aux` and `.bbl`, and `pandoc` runs without `--citeproc` for it; the criterion is the year in a `\bibcite` line, in place of the ruling's "no numbers in the `.aux`": round-1 ruling 24, ruling 9, handed back above.
13. The DOCX layout is written into a reference document by `templates/reference_docx.py` from the format profile, in place of academic-paper's prose style map; it does not carry a caption placement, which `pandoc` fixes, and says so in its output: ruling 10 to 13 (ruling BB).
14. A `.bib` given with a citation-style target gets its reference list in IEEE, Chicago author-date or Vancouver from the style's `.bst`, and in any other style, MLA and Chicago notes included, only from a CSL file among the materials in hand; a style with neither gets no list and the final message says so: ruling 20.
15. The compile engine is the first that applies of a `% !TEX program =` line, the class table, the venue's template and XeLaTeX, in place of `tectonic` or `xelatex`: round-1 ruling 25, rulings 5 and 6. The engine of `svjour3` and `mdpi` is not verified.
16. The content check counts headings, figures, tables, table rows and columns, cite commands against cited entries, figure paths and the abstract's first words, with no threshold, in place of the one percent word-count threshold, and the session appends the commands it ran to the report: the brief's item 6, rulings 21, 23 and 26.
17. The formatter checks the supplementary-materials format and the separate-figure-files requirement and lists each in the report ("not stated" when neither the Venue limits row nor the template says so): ruling 22. The report has a Language row: ruling 24. A policy brief may gain sidebars and callout boxes: ruling 25. All three follow the source.
