## Argument blueprint

### Central thesis

This paper argues that, for label-free recovery of sinus rhythm (SR) against atrial fibrillation (AF) in the 12-lead ECG, the representation together with its feature conditioning decides whether clustering succeeds, while the choice among centroid, hierarchical, spectral and mixture clusterers moves a strong representation far less, because the five representations span an adjusted Rand index (ARI) of 0.000 to 0.885 where those four clusterers move ECGFounder and the hand-crafted features by at most 0.18, because supervised ceilings and an out-of-fold discriminant show that the low-scoring representations fail for different reasons that none of the five clusterers tested addresses, and because 16 hand-crafted features keep more of their structure than ECGFounder under 9:1 class imbalance and simulated site heterogeneity at one fifteenth of its extraction time, based on a chance-corrected, patient-grouped benchmark on PTB-XL with a replication on MIT-BIH beat morphology.

The thesis is specific (two tasks, five named representations, five clusterers, two stressors), arguable (a reader can hold that the clusterer, or the pretraining of the encoder, is the deciding choice, and image studies show that a learned clustering objective recovers more than k-means from the same features), supportable (every number is in manuscript/main.tex) and relevant (it answers both parts of the research question of config.md). The pattern is that of engineering: problem, approach, validation.

### Sub-arguments

#### Sub-argument 1: The representation with its conditioner governs clustering agreement, and the four non-density clusterers move it far less.
- **Evidence**: Table 1 of main.tex (lines 428 to 432): over k-means, Ward, spectral and GMM, ECGFounder spans 0.709 to 0.885 and the hand-crafted features 0.544 to 0.711, while the five representations span 0.000 to 0.885; patient-grouped intervals separate every pair of representations except the two controls (lines 414 to 416); the order holds for AMI at every cluster count from 2 to 8 (lines 461 to 464). The conditioner moves ECGFounder's mean ARI from 0.866 (standard) to 0.093 (robust) and reverses its order with the hand-crafted features (0.360) under robust scaling (lines 576 to 581). On MIT-BIH the order of the three representations applied repeats (lines 607 to 609). From the literature, the evaluation protocol changes which ECG representation ranks best [arxiv_2602_17531]; the scaler changes the k-means result [Wongoutong_2024]; and on CODE-15%, Georgia and Chapman-Shaoxing the AF label-level ARI of Gaussian mixture clusters is 0.70 for ECGFounder against 0.00 to 0.04 for three HuBERT-ECG sizes [lookingbeyond2026], the same order of the two models as here (GMM 0.851 against 0.195 on PTB-XL).
- **Reasoning**: If the clusterer were the deciding choice, the spread down a column of Table 1 would be no larger than the spread along a row. It is several times larger for every pair of the four non-density clusterers, and changing the conditioner moves agreement by as much as changing the representation, so the conditioner belongs to the representation in the claim. That an independent study on other datasets finds the same order of the two foundation models shows the order is a property of the two embeddings and not of this cohort alone.
- **Counter-argument**: The ranking is expected, because SR and AF differ by definition in RR regularity, and ECGFounder was pretrained on expert labels [ecgfounder2024], so its "label-free" clusters inherit supervision; HDBSCAN moves ECGFounder from 0.885 to 0.137, a spread as large as the one across representations; and on pretrained image features a clustering objective learned on mined neighbours raises accuracy from 65.9 to 87.6 percent over k-means on the same features [Van_Gansbeke_2020], with gains of 6.1 and 12.2 points across 17 models [arxiv_2303_17896], so a better clusterer could close the gap.
- **Rebuttal**: Concede and limit. The paper states in 1.4 that the ranking follows from the class definitions and that the contribution is what the controls and stressors show; the clustering step on the target cohort uses no labels, and ECGFounder's supervised pretraining is the mechanism Discussion 5.1 gives for its alignment. The clusterer statement is scoped to the four non-density clusterers, and HDBSCAN's position is attributed to its noise labelling at the minimum cluster size used (main.tex lines 342 to 352). Learned clustering objectives are outside the five clusterers tested and are named in 5.4; the image results agree with sub-argument 2, since they show class structure present in features that k-means does not follow, which is what the out-of-fold test shows for HuBERT-ECG.

#### Sub-argument 2: Low agreement has different causes in different representations: missing linear structure in raw flattening, an off-axis label in HuBERT-ECG and the conv-AE.
- **Evidence**: Linear ceilings against best ARI: raw 0.519 against 0.013, conv-AE 0.678 against 0.008, HuBERT-ECG 0.899 against 0.195 (Table 1). Random-forest ceilings: raw 0.714, HuBERT-ECG 0.825, ECGFounder 0.983, hand-crafted 0.951 (lines 506 to 512). The out-of-fold discriminant test raises held-out ARI from 0.160 to 0.549 for HuBERT-ECG, 0.003 to 0.121 for the conv-AE, 0.646 to 0.758 for the hand-crafted features, 0.893 to 0.923 for ECGFounder, and leaves raw flattening at 0.000 to 0.001 (lines 521 to 524). From the literature: for frozen self-supervised image representations, linear-probe and nearest-centroid error depend on the variance along class-separating directions, which can be small while most within-class variance lies in other directions [arxiv_2603_03530]; linear-probe accuracy can stay unaffected while partition-based retrieval degrades [arxiv_2604_24469]; k-means under-recovers class structure the same features hold [Van_Gansbeke_2020; arxiv_2303_17896]; HuBERT-ECG embeddings organise by dataset more than by clinical label [lookingbeyond2026]; plain autoencoder latents of images show large inter-cluster overlap [Wheeldon_2023]; ECG autoencoder latents carry clinical information shown by supervised association [Friedman_2025; Jang_2021].
- **Reasoning**: A direction fitted on other patients that raises held-out agreement shows label information that is present, transfers across patients and lies off the dominant axes; HuBERT-ECG gains 3.4 times. That is the configuration the image literature describes, a label direction with small variance beside large variance in other directions, which a partition over all directions does not follow. Raw flattening gains nothing from that direction while a random forest decodes 0.714, so its label information is non-linear and no single direction separates the classes. ECGFounder gains little because its dominant axes already carry the split, which agrees with its supervised pretraining [ecgfounder2024].
- **Counter-argument**: A linear ceiling understates what a non-linear space holds, so calling any failure "geometric" or "informational" depends on the probe; the autoencoder literature reports ECG latents that cluster by rhythm [Jang_2021]; and the sources for the mechanism concern image representations.
- **Rebuttal**: Concede and limit, then refute in part. The paper reports both probes and reads raw flattening as non-linear information without a linear arrangement, the reading the random forest supports; for HuBERT-ECG the random forest sits below the linear ceiling, so probe linearity does not explain its gap. Jang_2021 shows rhythm structure in a variational autoencoder trained on 596,000 ICU samples through supervised associations and latent-space exploration, and not through agreement of unsupervised clusters with rhythm labels (review.md, synthesis T2); the conv-AE here keeps 0.678 of the label and gains out of fold, which agrees with information present and diffuse. The image sources are cited for the mechanism and its precedent; the ECG measurement is this paper's own, made out of fold on held-out patients.

#### Sub-argument 3: Chance-corrected external measures with a cluster-size check are required, because internal indices and purity reward a degenerate partition.
- **Evidence**: Raw flattening with Ward clustering reaches a silhouette of 0.81 with a largest-cluster fraction of 1.00 and an ARI of 0.000, while the hand-crafted features reach a silhouette of 0.28 at an ARI of 0.711 (lines 550 to 554); a single cluster scores a purity of 0.504 (Table metrics, line 335). From the literature, classification rates should not be used to validate clusters [Steinley_2004], ARI is corrected for chance [hubertarabie1985], AMI is the measure for an unbalanced reference [arxiv_1512_01286], and the silhouette is an internal index of tightness and separation [rousseeuw1987]. Internal measures are unreliable for comparing partitions computed in different embedding spaces [Wang_2026], and some validation measures do not register the uniform effect of k-means [Hui_Xiong_2009]. Applied ECG clustering uses the silhouette to set the cluster count for autoencoder embeddings of 96,921 ECGs [Shetty_2024] and as the only validation on an unlabelled wearable heartbeat database [Digiacomo_2025].
- **Reasoning**: An index that ranks the partition with no class information above the partition that recovers the classes cannot serve as validation alone; the comparison in 4.3 is between partitions in two different representation spaces, the case Wang_2026 identifies as unreliable. The largest-cluster fraction exposes the degenerate partition, and ARI with AMI scores it at zero.
- **Counter-argument**: The silhouette is internal by definition and was never meant to measure label agreement, so the caution argues against a use no careful study makes.
- **Rebuttal**: Refute in part, then concede and limit. Applied ECG studies use the silhouette to choose and support a partition where no labels exist [Shetty_2024; Digiacomo_2025], which is the setting in which a degenerate partition cannot be detected by agreement. The paper states the silhouette's definition, presents the result as a specific, reproducible case, and makes no statement about the results of those studies. Purity as validation rests on Steinley_2004 and the single-cluster value of 0.504; no applied ECG source reporting purity was found.

#### Sub-argument 4: The 16 hand-crafted features are the more robust representation under class imbalance and simulated site heterogeneity in prevalence, and the cheaper one to run.
- **Evidence**: At 9:1 SR:AF over ten seeds, hand-crafted ARI 0.265 [0.250, 0.280] and AMI 0.245 [0.233, 0.257] against ECGFounder ARI 0.063 [0.061, 0.065] and AMI 0.148 [0.143, 0.152] (lines 620 to 626). Under non-IID federation the hand-crafted features retain 0.92 of their centralized ARI against 0.69 for ECGFounder; their lowest of ten site draws is 0.455, while three ECGFounder draws fall to 0.132 or below and one to chance (lines 633 to 637, 652 to 655). Extraction costs 28.3 ms per record against 434.7 ms, with 364.0 MB against 1,037.6 MB of peak memory (lines 703 to 707). From the literature, AMI is the measure for an unbalanced reference [arxiv_1512_01286]; k-means-type algorithms tend to clusters of relatively uniform size when the true sizes differ [Hui_Xiong_2009; Liang_2012], which bears on the fall common to all representations at 9:1 if the clusterer of the imbalance result is k-means; federated averaging degrades under Dirichlet partitions [arxiv_1909_06335]; foundation-model cost is profiled among encoders only [arxiv_2608_03597; arxiv_2509_25095]; siloed clinical data motivates federation [Rieke_2020].
- **Reasoning**: Under imbalance the hand-crafted features lead under both ARI and the measure suited to the unbalanced reference, with no interval overlap. The uniform effect explains why every representation falls and does not explain the order, which is the comparison the claim rests on, made with the clusterer held fixed. Under federation the means are close (0.591 against 0.612), and the claim rests on retention and on the worst site draw, the quantity a deployment depends on. The cost comparison places the representation that holds up under both stressors at one fifteenth of the time and about a third of the memory.
- **Counter-argument**: ECGFounder keeps the higher mean non-IID ARI (0.612 against 0.591); the federation is a simulation on one dataset whose sites differ only in prevalence; FOUND-AF finds ECGFounder favourable in accuracy against cost [arxiv_2608_03597]; heterogeneity can help federated clustering [arxiv_2103_00697]; a multicentre clusterer counters the uniform effect [Liang_2012] and might change the imbalance result; ECGFounder has a single-lead extension [ecgfounder2024].
- **Rebuttal**: Concede and limit, and acknowledge as a limitation. The robustness claim is stated as retention and worst-case agreement, with the means reported beside it; it is scoped to simulated prevalence heterogeneity on PTB-XL, with real institution splits named as the next study [Perez_Alday_2020; Reyna_2021]. FOUND-AF's cost comparison is among foundation models and does not include hand-crafted features. k-FED is a one-shot method with a separation assumption and was not the method tested. Multicentre k-means is outside the five clusterers tested and is named in 5.4. The lead-count clause of the draft is not carried, since no reduced-lead foundation-model run was made.

### Logical flow

- Introduction: problem (label-free ECG analysis depends on the representation; probe accuracy and partition diverge in image representations), gap (frozen encoders scored through supervised heads, the closest study compares foundation models with each other, no hand-crafted comparison under clustering), purpose and questions (which representation, which failure, which robustness), contributions and scope.
- Related work: context (representation families), theme 1 (hand-crafted features), theme 2 (self-supervised and foundation encoders and their benchmarks, with the label-level and dataset-level ARI of the closest study), theme 3 (unsupervised ECG studies, separability and clusterability with its mechanism, federated clustering), theme 4 (evaluation measures, the reliability of internal indices, statistics), gap confirmed.
- Methods: approach justified (controls, ceilings, the conditioner sweep), data described (cohorts and arrays), representations defined with their conditioners and lead sets, analysis explained (clusterers, metrics with the cluster-size check, ceilings, out-of-fold test, intervals, effect sizes, federation).
- Results: agreement by representation, conditioner and MIT-BIH (supports sub-argument 1); ceilings, random forest and out-of-fold test (supports sub-argument 2); silhouette and purity (supports sub-argument 3); imbalance, federation and cost (supports sub-argument 4).
- Discussion: interpretation of each representation with the mechanism from the literature (sub-argument 2 and the counter-arguments of sub-argument 1), the conditioner, spectral and imbalance readings, comparison with the closest study and the benchmarks, the silhouette caution against applied practice (sub-argument 3), bounded implications (sub-argument 4 with its counter-arguments), limitations as scope boundaries (two models, five clusterers, one lead for the hand-crafted features, two binary tasks, simulated federation).
- Conclusion: thesis restated within the two tasks, the two models, the five clusterers and the simulated federation; open questions.

Unsupported claims, with no evidence in review.md or main.tex, each kept out of the argument or worded as the paper's own choice or reading (outline.md, "Needs literature"): that lead II is the standard rhythm lead and that the single-lead choice is a clinical convention (item 3); that robust scaling is a common default for physiological signals, and that heavy-tailed features dominate Euclidean distance after it (item 4); that internal indices are maximized by degenerate partitions as a general statement (item 6); that applied ECG clustering reports purity as validation. One claim lacks a measurement, not a source: that the nearest-neighbour graph of the spectral clustering is poorly connected on HuBERT-ECG, which main.tex does not report.

### Weak-argument signs

The signs below are found in the draft's wording of the four core arguments. Sub-arguments 1, 2 and 4 each hold two or more signs, which stops the drafting; the session shows them to the user at the outline's approval. Sub-argument 3 holds one sign, which is noted and does not stop the drafting. The planned wording of each sub-argument above is built to remove every sign listed, and in that wording no sign was found; whether the remedies are accepted is the user's decision at that stop.

Sub-argument 1, in the draft's wording "The representation, not the algorithm, governs label-free ECG structure" (main.tex lines 84 to 85, the heading of 4.1, lines 403 to 405, 800 to 801):
- **A key term undefined and used inconsistently.** "Representation" excludes the conditioner in draft 4.4, where "the feature scaler changes the outcome materially" (lines 576 to 583), and the Conclusion names "the representation and the evaluation protocol" as the deciding choices (lines 810 to 811). Remedy planned: a representation is defined as a feature map with its conditioner in 1.3 and 3.2.
- **A false dichotomy.** The draft opposes representation to algorithm, while a third choice, the conditioner, moves ECGFounder from 0.866 to 0.093 and reverses its order with the hand-crafted features, and HDBSCAN moves ECGFounder from 0.885 to 0.137. Remedy planned: the thesis names the conditioner, the clusterer statement is scoped to the four non-density clusterers, and the reversal is reported in 4.1.2.
- **A counter-argument stronger than the argument.** The draft says changing the clustering algorithm "addresses neither" failure (lines 489 to 490) and that the algorithm does not govern the structure, with no clusterer outside the five tested, while on pretrained image features a clustering objective learned on mined neighbours raises accuracy over k-means from 65.9 to 87.6 percent on the same features [Van_Gansbeke_2020], and a clustering head gains 6.1 and 12.2 points across 17 models [arxiv_2303_17896]. Against the unscoped claim that evidence is the stronger one. Remedy planned: every clusterer statement, the thesis included, is scoped to the five clusterers tested, and learned clustering objectives are named in 5.4.

Sub-argument 2, in the draft's explanation of the ranking (main.tex lines 117 to 118, 480 to 481, 487 to 489, 496 to 497, 504 to 505):
- **Circular reasoning.** "ECGFounder ... recovers the most ... so the rhythm split is one of the larger sources of variation in its 1024 dimensions" explains the agreement by a restatement of it. Remedy planned: the out-of-fold gain (0.893 to 0.923) and the supervised pretraining [ecgfounder2024] carry the explanation as evidence independent of the agreement.
- **A key term used inconsistently.** "Label information" means linear decodability where the draft says raw flattening fails "for want of label information in the space" and "carries almost no label information", and the random forest decodes 0.714 from the same space (line 506). Remedy planned: raw flattening is read as non-linear information without a linear arrangement.
- **An appeal to authority without evidence.** The draft calls the divergence "the established separability-versus-clusterability effect" and "established in the self-supervised learning literature" with sslclustering2025 as the only citation, whose annotation reports clustering properties of self-supervised components and no comparison with linear probes. Remedy planned: the credit goes to arxiv_2603_03530, arxiv_2604_24469, Van_Gansbeke_2020 and arxiv_2303_17896, which report the divergence and its mechanism for image representations, with sslclustering2025 as background.

Sub-argument 3, in the draft's wording of the silhouette caution (main.tex lines 175 to 177, 737 to 738):
- **An appeal to authority without evidence.** The draft cites rousseeuw1987 for internal indices that "can be maximised by degenerate solutions", which its annotation does not state, and says both artefacts "recur often enough in applied clustering" with no source. Remedy planned: the degenerate case is stated as this paper's result, Wang_2026 and Hui_Xiong_2009 carry the unreliability of internal measures, and Shetty_2024 and Digiacomo_2025 carry the applied use of the silhouette. One sign; it does not stop the drafting.

Sub-argument 4, in the draft's deployment reading (main.tex lines 620 to 621, 640 to 641, 716 to 722, 727 to 729):
- **A hasty generalization.** One simulated partition scheme on one dataset, varying prevalence only, is generalized to "privacy-preserving, multi-site phenotyping"; and the imbalance fall is attributed to "a partitional method" in general, where the sources state the uniform effect for k-means-type algorithms [Hui_Xiong_2009; Liang_2012] and main.tex does not name the clusterer. Remedy planned: the claim is scoped to simulated prevalence heterogeneity and states retention and worst-case agreement; the imbalance explanation is scoped to k-means-type algorithms, conditional on the clusterer, and moved to Discussion 5.1.
- **A counter-argument stronger than the argument.** The draft says the two foundation models "cannot embed" non-twelve-lead data and recommends the hand-crafted features "if the leads on hand are not the standard twelve", with no reduced-lead measurement, while ECGFounder is extended to lower-lead and single-lead ECGs [ecgfounder2024]. Remedy planned: the lead-count clause is not carried, and the MIT-BIH omission is stated by its input unit, 10 s strips against 550 ms beats, for the checkpoints used.

### Notes for the draft writer

- **Scope in every claim.** Each statement about a foundation model names HuBERT-ECG or ECGFounder; each statement about clusterers names the five tested; each general sentence carries its task (SR against AF on PTB-XL, N-vs-V on MIT-BIH) and, for federation, "simulated" and "prevalence". This is what Reviewer 3, item 8, asks for.
- **Observation and interpretation.** Results state measurements; "because", "so" and "which shows" belong to the Discussion. The draft's explanations inside Results 4.1.2 and 4.4.1 (main.tex lines 580 to 582, 620 to 621) move to 5.1.
- **Hedging.** Mechanism readings in 5.1 are worded as readings of the ceiling and the out-of-fold test ("is consistent with", "places the split on its dominant axes"); the heavy-tail reading of robust scaling and the graph-connectivity reading of the spectral cell are worded as possible causes the study does not test; measured differences are stated without hedging, with their intervals.
- **Literature from other domains.** The separability-and-clusterability sources, Wheeldon_2023 and Wongoutong_2024 concern image or generic data; each citation says so in its sentence, and the paper claims the ECG measurement as its own.
- **The closest study.** Compare the order of ECGFounder and HuBERT-ECG with [lookingbeyond2026], never the magnitudes: its ARI pools other datasets, has no interval, and does not cover AF on PTB-XL. State what its text omits (hand-crafted features, MIT-BIH) as an observation about the source, never as its own statement.
- **Applied studies.** Shetty_2024 and Digiacomo_2025 are cited as examples of the use of the silhouette; nothing is said about whether their partitions are degenerate.
- **One term per concept.** "Representation" for a feature map with its conditioner; "conditioner" for the scaler; "hand-crafted features" for the 16-feature set and "beat-morphology features" for the 21-feature MIT-BIH set; "supervised ceiling" for the probe accuracy; "raw flattening" and "conv-AE" for the two controls. The manuscript prose standard, section G, rules out "pipeline" for the project's own arms, which the draft uses throughout.
- **Self-assessment.** Words such as "careful", "honest" and "modest" about the study (main.tex lines 109, 126, 136, 734) are cut; the contribution statement and the scope carry that content.
- **The earlier master's project.** No paragraph is built on it; the raw-flattening arm is described as a control of whole-recording flattening.
- **Numbers.** Three decimals for ARI and AMI and two for retention (Reviewer 3, item 13); sample sizes in every caption (item 11); differences taken from unrounded seed means, stated once; the three Wilcoxon contrasts named as tested.
- **Prose standard.** No em dash or spaced-hyphen aside; at most two semicolons per 1,000 words of running text, which the draft exceeds in several paragraphs; headings as labels; the binary "not X but Y" construction at most twice in the paper, where the draft uses it in its abstract, two Results headings and its Conclusion; no closing sentence that restates its paragraph, such as the abstract's last sentence.
