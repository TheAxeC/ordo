# Intended claims

The claims that the text added or changed by the revision patch of `main.tex` makes, written before the patch from items W1 to W49.

1. On both tasks evaluated, the spread of clustering ARI across representations was larger than the spread across the clustering algorithms applied to one representation. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
2. The order of the representations follows from the class definitions, since SR and AF differ by definition in rhythm regularity, and is not the paper's contribution. | evidence: definitional | sources: the project's results
3. On PTB-XL, ECGFounder reached the highest clustering ARI (0.885) and the 16 hand-crafted features reached 0.711, while the raw and conv-AE pipelines matched a majority-class assignment under all five clusterers. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
4. On MIT-BIH, the 21-dimensional beat-morphology feature set clustered well (ARI 0.783), whereas the conv-AE reached at most 0.127 and the raw beat window at most 0.049. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
5. The conv-AE's supervised ceiling (0.678) is well above the majority baseline of 0.504, while the raw-flattening ceiling (0.519) is barely above it. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
6. Raw flattening holds almost no linearly recoverable label information, although a random forest on the same features reaches 0.714. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
7. On the representations that cluster well, the non-linear probe adds little over the linear ceiling, so their clustering is not limited by the linearity of the information. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
8. The misalignment of fixed sample offsets with the cardiac cycle is a likely cause of the raw arm's linear failure, not a demonstrated one. | evidence: theoretical | sources: the project's results
9. The twelve-lead input of the raw arm makes its vector 1992-dimensional, which may itself bear on Euclidean clustering, so the lead set may still affect the raw arm's clustering through dimensionality. | evidence: theoretical | sources: the project's results
10. Under the 9:1 SR:AF subsample, at one ratio and one feature scaling, the hand-crafted features overtook ECGFounder (ARI 0.265 against 0.063). | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
11. Under that subsample HuBERT-ECG reached an ARI similar to ECGFounder's at a lower AMI, so its partition shared less information with the classes. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
12. Under non-IID federation the hand-crafted features retained 0.92 of their centralized ARI against 0.69 for ECGFounder, and their worst site partition returned 0.455, while three of ECGFounder's ten partitions fell to 0.132 or below. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
13. Under non-IID federation ECGFounder's ARI fell from 0.885 to 0.612, and the two means were close (0.612 against 0.591). | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
14. In the simulated setting on PTB-XL, the hand-crafted features retained more of their clustering ARI under both stressors than ECGFounder did. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
15. Hand-crafted extraction costs fifteen times less per record than ECGFounder and needs about a third of its resident memory. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
16. Feature scaling changes the result: the hand-crafted features' mean ARI fell from 0.629 under standard scaling to 0.229 under quantile scaling, and ECGFounder's from 0.866 to 0.093 under robust scaling. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
17. For a one-cluster solution ARI and AMI are zero, while the silhouette can still be high. | evidence: definitional | sources: hubertarabie1985, vinh2010, rousseeuw1987
18. A reconstruction objective rewards signal energy and does not ask the bottleneck to separate classes, so a reconstruction autoencoder may encode class information without arranging it into clusters. | evidence: theoretical | sources: the project's results
19. Spectral clustering fell on some representations, possibly because the nearest-neighbour affinity graph was poorly connected; connectivity was not measured. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
20. Isolating a 10% minority cluster may be harder for a partitional method, which may explain part of the general fall under imbalance. | evidence: theoretical | sources: the project's results
21. The 21-dimensional MIT-BIH feature set is a separate beat-level feature set of the same kind as the 16 PTB-XL features, not the same features. | evidence: definitional | sources: the project's results
22. Atrial flutter records were counted as AF positives, and flutter need not show the irregular rhythm on which the reading of the SR-versus-AF result rests. | evidence: definitional | sources: ptbxl2020
23. HuBERT-ECG received 5 s of each 10 s strip, so its shorter input window is an alternative explanation for its weak clustering. | evidence: theoretical | sources: the project's results
24. The patient-bootstrap intervals hold the partitions fixed and so leave out the variation from refitting the clusterer on a resampled cohort. | evidence: definitional | sources: the project's results
25. In the federated simulation feature scaling used statistics pooled over all sites, which separate sites would not share. | evidence: definitional | sources: the project's results
26. On a 300-record subset the upsampled 100 Hz input gave ECGFounder an ARI 0.013 higher than the native 500 Hz input, so the main benchmark may overstate ECGFounder's ARI by about that amount. | evidence: empirical | sources: the project's results
27. The worst site draw is the deployment criterion this study uses, because a deployment meets a single site partition. | evidence: normative | sources: the project's results
