### Lit-review

1. An unread candidate is looked for at another index before it is dropped (`references/literature-strategist.md`, "Screening"). A candidate whose abstract and text the strategist could not read with the lookups its page names is resolved again with `lookup.py resolve` on each identifier it has (its DOI, its arXiv ID, its title), so each index that holds it gives its record and its identifiers. A candidate still unread is screened on its record and its `cited-by` count: a work layer 2 marked "must include", or a foundational work under the seminal rule of the screening tree, is included and annotated "abstract not read", with the lookups tried; any other unread candidate is excluded with those lookups named in `review.md`. No candidate is excluded only because its text could not be read. Lit-review fetches no full text (ADR 0024), and no lookup leaves `lookup.py` (ADR 0017).
2. A work with no DOI or arXiv ID gets a `.bib` entry (`literature-strategist.md`, "The BibTeX entries"). The entry is written from the fields of the record `lookup.py resolve '<title>'` prints for it (title, authors, year, venue, and the record's open-access URL or index page), in the type its venue gives (`@inproceedings` for a proceedings volume, `@article` for a journal, `@misc` otherwise), copied from that printed line and never typed from memory, then confirmed with item 18's `resolve --entry`. A source no index prints a record for keeps the "No entry written" listing.
3. A venue or a year is confirmed only by an index that answered (`literature-strategist.md`; `references/citation-compliance.md`, "Looking a reference up"). An index line `left out, <reason>` confirms nothing. The venue and the year of an entry are taken from an index that printed a record; an entry whose year or venue only a left-out index could have given is resolved again by its DOI or arXiv ID, which reaches Crossref and OpenAlex, and one that no answering index confirms is named in the report and `review.md` with the indexes left out.
4. The search's end, as ruling BI 4 (a) decides (`literature-strategist.md`, "The four searches"): the search ends when at least 3 of the 5 conditions hold and condition 2 (the latest pass added fewer than 10 percent of the existing sources) is among them; a pass that adds 10 percent or more always runs another, up to 8 passes, after which a "search limitation" names the conditions that hold; with no condition holding after 4 passes the search ends with a "search limitation", as academic-paper's rule says. The report names each pass with the sources it added. Every statement of the cap is rewritten to this rule: `SKILL.md` lit-review item 1, full items 1 and 5, revision item 3, and `literature-strategist.md:110`, `:120`, `:305`, `:323`, `:333`.
