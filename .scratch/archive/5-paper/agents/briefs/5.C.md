## What to build

### 1. Lit-review reads its sources

- `lookup.py fetch` is among the literature strategist's commands in every mode: the role table (`SKILL.md:750`) and every place its page names the strategist's commands.
- `fetch` also takes a Semantic Scholar ID and an OpenAlex ID, read as `refs` and `cited-by` read them:
  - The one index the ID belongs to is asked for the work's record.
  - When the record gives a DOI or an arXiv ID, `fetch` goes on as for that input.
  - Otherwise it tries that index's open-access URL and writes that index's abstract when no full text is found.
  - The usage line, the head comment's `fetch` bullet and the row of `docs/dev/scripts.md` that lists `fetch`'s inputs say so.
- When the strategist fetches:
  - At the stage "Title and abstract", a candidate the title alone excludes is not fetched.
  - Every other candidate is fetched into the run's temporary folder before its abstract is read, and is screened and annotated from the `<stem>.txt` or `<stem>.abstract.txt` that `fetch` writes, read with the Read tool.
  - Node 3 of the screening tree (`literature-strategist.md:144`) and the annotation rule (`:216`) read that file.
- The input of each `fetch` is the candidate's DOI, else its arXiv ID, else its Semantic Scholar ID, else its OpenAlex ID. The key is the same identifier, so no two candidates share a stem.
- Exit 1 of `fetch` with `<stem>.abstract.txt` written is the abstract case: the candidate is screened from the abstract.
  - The same holds when `pdftotext` is missing: `fetch` goes on to the next URL and then to the abstract.
- A source given as a `.bib` entry is screened from its `abstract` field when it has one, and is fetched otherwise.
- `literature-strategist.md:138` loses "and lit-review mode fetches no full text".
- The block "A candidate that cannot be read" applies only to a candidate whose `fetch` wrote neither file, or that has none of the four identifiers.
  - `review.md` names the URLs `fetch` printed for it, or "no identifier to fetch".
- The temporary folder:
  - `SKILL.md` Steps 7 makes it in lit-review and outline-only too, and in full and revision whenever the strategist starts.
  - It is passed to the strategist, used for its fetched texts and removed at the run's end as now.
  - The lead sentence of Steps (`SKILL.md:84`) names the modes Steps 7 runs in.
- ADR 0024's Consequences sentence on lit-review is edited to say that a lit-review run fetches the candidates' texts for screening only, and that no claim is judged from them.

### 3. The draft review decides after round 2 as academic-paper does

- An overall score of 6.5 or more: the run goes on, and the issues left go to the final message as acknowledged limitations.
- Below 6.5 with every Critical issue resolved: the run goes on, with the issues left as acknowledged limitations.
- Below 6.5 with a Critical issue unresolved: the stop "The draft review does not pass".
