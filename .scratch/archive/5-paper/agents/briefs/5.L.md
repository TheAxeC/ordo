## What to build

### 1. Each role's prompt written for its task

`SKILL.md` "## Agent prompts" says that the session writes each role agent's prompt for the task in front of it, as academic-paper's orchestrating session writes its agents' briefs, in every mode and for every start and every second message. The fixed pieces of lines 796 to 813 stay, as what every prompt carries at least. To them the session adds, in its own words for this run:

- the run's specifics the task turns on: for a manuscript, the files, the results and the numbers each part rests on, the definitions and the names the paper uses, each fixed once; for a search, the request's own strands;
- what the agent checks in particular for this manuscript or this request, named for it: for a reviewer, the claims, numbers and wordings most at risk in this draft; for a writer, what each section must hold; for the citation compliance agent, the sources and claims most at risk;
- the sentence that a shortcut (a step of its page done in part, a source not read, a count not observed) is a defect, not a saving.

The prompt never narrows the role's page: what the session adds is direction inside the page's rules, never a rule that removes one. Nothing it adds replaces a fixed piece.

### 2. The theme run's, merge run's and single strategist's prompts

"### The prompts of a literature search" keeps its lists as the minimum, and says what the session adds to a theme run's prompt from the request and the theme:

- the foundational works of the theme, named, with any year admitted for them through the page's path for seminal works, and the period of recent work the theme needs;
- the question each method of the theme is read for, taken from the research question;
- the annotation fields that answer the sub-questions the research question asks, beside the page's own fields (the glossary's **part, of a research question** is the `config.md` row the strategist does not read, and this text does not use that term);
- the databases and venues the topic needs, beside those of the page's discipline table;
- the sub-topics of the theme, each with a minimum of included sources, and that a sub-topic with fewer after a documented search is reported with its searches, never filled by a weaker source.

The single strategist's prompt carries the same additions over the whole request. The merge run's prompt carries the run's specifics and what the merge checks in particular for this request (each part of the research question answered across the themes, the foundational works each theme named kept). `references/literature-strategist.md:3` names that its prompt gives this direction.
