# 0036. Paper sets no limit on agent starts, follows academic-paper's draft-review and patch stops, and records each deviation from it

Status: proposed; superseded by 0037 in its sentence that the session decides when to start a role agent fresh and how many run at once, for the literature strategist's search for the review in full, outline-only and lit-review mode; refined by 0040 in its deviation on ASCII entries, which the literature strategist carries to the entries it writes itself; that refinement ended by 0042; superseded by the ruling The academic-paper strategist of plan 5 in its deviations for the literature strategist: the databases for each field, the layer-3 and layer-4 sources named "Not reached" with OpenCitations in their place, the question split into parts with a string each, each synthesis sentence checked against its annotation, the preprint label after `lookup.py resolve`, the widening by 5 years and the currency passes, and the ASCII entries as far as the strategist writes them; restored by 0042 in its sentence that the session decides the starts, for the literature search; superseded by 0043 in its starts, now academic-paper's, the starts of the literature search standing by 0042, in its draft-review stops, now academic-paper's round-2 decision and its stop after round 2, in its one stop for every claim the run cannot support, now academic-paper's stop at a claim not in its source, and in its deviations the anonymous copy and the palette outline, the caps on a fix, the pdfLaTeX engine and the ASCII entries of `lookup.py bibtex` standing

## Context

ADR 0027 decides that the literature strategist and the visualization agent "are each started once as a fresh agent", lists the only cases in which a role is resumed, and ends with "No other agent receives a second message"; ADR 0029 keeps that sentence. academic-paper 3.2.0 sets no such limit and leaves to its session when an agent is started or resumed. Ruling Open item BR of plan 5 (`.scratch/5-paper/plan.md`, Rulings) removes every limit on starts that academic-paper does not set, and asks that `paper` follow academic-paper wherever the ledger had called a point equivalent that was not.

academic-paper's draft review decides after round 2 by the score: 6.5 or more goes on, below 6.5 with every Critical issue resolved goes on with the issues left as acknowledged limitations (`agents/peer_reviewer_agent.md:427`), and below 6.5 with a Critical issue unresolved stops with the answers revise by hand and resubmit, lower the venue, and accept. A review that does not pass after round 2 gets a root cause analysis with an estimate of the additional work and the answers accept, an expanded revision and end (`agents/peer_reviewer_agent.md:424-452`). Its verdict table makes every score below 6.5 a Major Revision or a Reject (`:67`-`:68`), so the root cause analysis is reached at a score below 6.5 with a Critical issue unresolved; a Reject is F7, whose handling classes each Critical issue and writes a roadmap before any revision (`references/failure_paths.md:160-186`). Neither round-2 stop recommends an answer; F7 recommends a return for each class (`references/failure_paths.md:168-185`). `paper`'s stop "The draft review does not pass" recommended accept, offered three answers in every case, and a round-1 Reject reached the same stop. academic-paper's patch protocol answers a second rejection with a retry, a full re-emission the user confirms, or abort (`references/revision_patch_protocol.md:40-60`); `paper` offered one more try.

Two reports give the findings this record answers. The simulation `.scratch/5-paper/agents/reviews/5-simulation.md` judged the text of the two skills, not a run's output; it found that the citation check stops at the first claim marked "Not held", so the user sees one claim at a time (F1), and that in lit-review the source-count failure path neither searches the widened years nor adds indexes (F2). The two judges of the blind comparison of the lit-review outputs on `v8.4.0-rc.6` (`.scratch/5-paper/agents/reviews/5.D-check-blind-comparison/lit-review/judgment-1.md` and `judgment-2.md`) found in `paper`'s review a synthesis sentence that cites a survey for "one EHR study", published papers labelled as preprints, a recency share of 45.2 percent below its 50 percent target, 75 lines of `refs.bib` with characters outside ASCII, and thin coverage of the clinical side of the question; both judges credited the side whose synthesis follows the three parts of the research question.

The builder of step 5.F probed academic-paper's two remaining search tools on the DOI `10.1038/nature14539`. `https://www.scopus.com` answered 403 with the page "Attention Required!", a bot check. `https://www.connectedpapers.com` answered 200 with a page of scripts that holds no title of a related work, and `rest.connectedpapers.com` does not resolve. OpenCitations' index, `https://api.opencitations.net/index/v2/citations/doi:10.1038/nature14539`, answered 200 with records whose `citing` field names each citing work by its DOI (`.scratch/5-paper/agents/reviews/5.F-report.md`).

## Decision

**Starts.** The session decides when to start a role agent fresh, when to resume one with a second message, and how many run at once. A fresh start of a role that wrote files before in the run is given those files with its new input. The caps on a fix stay: the narrowing of a refused structural patch, the reference fix and the compile fix, and the second start of the citation compliance agent after a failing check. This supersedes ADR 0027 in its counts of starts and its list of resumptions, and ADR 0029 in its sentence that "No other agent receives a second message" stands.

**Draft review.** After draft review round 2 a score of 6.5 or more goes on, and so does a score below 6.5 with every Critical issue resolved, the issues left becoming acknowledged limitations. A score below 6.5 with a Critical issue unresolved is the stop "The draft review does not pass", which shows the root cause and the estimate of the additional work and offers each answer of academic-paper's two lists once: revise by hand and resubmit, lower the venue, accept, an expanded revision, and end the run. No answer is marked as recommended. A hand revision is copied before the edit, anchorized again, and checked by the incremental citation check on each changed paragraph with a cite command before a fresh round-2 review; a lower venue is recorded in the Venue row and a fresh reviewer scores the same draft against it. A verdict Reject in round 1 is the stop "The draft review rejects the draft": each Critical issue classed as fixable, structural or fundamental, a roadmap, the recommendation for each class, and no revision before the user confirms one. Both stops stay stops under an instruction not to stop.

**Patches.** At the stop "A patch that does not apply" the user chooses a retry after a fresh anchorize, as often as the user asks, the change written again in full as one patch over every block of the manifest, applied with `--acknowledge-structural` on the user's answer and followed by a fresh anchorize, or the change left not applied.

**Deviations from academic-paper, each kept with its reason:**

- The literature strategist selects two or three primary databases for each field the Discipline row names, where academic-paper selects them "by discipline": a topic across fields is searched in each field's databases.
- Scopus "Cited by" and Connected Papers are named "Not reached" with the reasons the probe gave; OpenCitations' open index takes the place of Scopus "Cited by" in layer 3. This supersedes ADR 0032's "The strategist's search reaches what academic-paper's reaches": the strategist searches where academic-paper's does except each database named "Not reached", with its reason.
- The research question is split into its parts before the search design, each part with its own search string, its sources named in the search record, its coverage held to the matrix gate, and its own part of the synthesis, which says where nothing was found. academic-paper searches the question as one and organises the synthesis by theme.
- A short source count widens the years by 5 and runs layers 1 to 4 again, then searches each unused open index, before "limited literature available" is written with each step named. A short currency share takes up to 3 passes over the last 3 years, ending when the share is met or a pass adds no new eligible source. academic-paper handles each failed gate once.
- Each cited sentence of the synthesis is checked against the source's annotation or saved text before it is written, a survey cited as a survey. This supersedes ADR 0027's sentence that lit-review runs check no claim, for the sentences of the strategist's own synthesis. A source is labelled a preprint only after its published version was looked for by `lookup.py resolve` on its title, its arXiv ID and a DOI an index names.
- Every entry `lookup.py bibtex` prints is ASCII, each character written in its LaTeX form, a `url` or `doi` percent-encoded, and a character with no form written `{?}` with a warning that the caller answers. academic-paper sets no character rule; the entry then compiles under pdfLaTeX and `bibtex` alike.
- The citation compliance agent judges every claim, and the session raises one stop that lists each claim not held, the sources not read shown apart with their addresses. academic-paper stops the check at the first claim not in its source (`agents/citation_compliance_agent.md:138`, "Major (claim not in source) -> STOP"); the user here rules on every such claim at once. This supersedes ADR 0024 in when a claim the run cannot support goes to the user, and ADR 0027 in its sentence that such a claim stops the run.
- pdfLaTeX compiles `elsarticle`, `IEEEtran` and `acmart`, where academic-paper compiles with `tectonic` or XeLaTeX: those classes set their Times fonts only under pdfLaTeX.
- The draft writer writes the anonymous copy for a double-blind venue, where academic-paper lists anonymization as a checklist item.
- The cyan, orange and grey of the palette are drawn only with a black outline, where academic-paper draws them without one under its own 3:1 contrast minimum, which those three fail against white.

**Outputs and arguments.** Full and revision copy the compiled PDF to `<main>.pdf` beside the main `.tex`, and full makes each DOCX or Markdown target of the row Target formats after the last compile by format-convert's steps. Two or more weak-argument signs in a core argument start a fresh planner that rewrites the argument before the outline's approval, which shows it before and after, as academic-paper returns such an argument to its argument builder. A claim with no citation is looked up in `review.md` and `refs.bib` before it is hedged or marked, as academic-paper's draft writer does.

## Alternatives rejected

- ADR 0027's limits kept: a role that a later input reaches after its context is gone cannot be given it, and the session pays for limits academic-paper does not set.
- The draft-review stop kept with accept recommended and three answers in every case: the user sees a recommendation academic-paper does not make, and a hand revision or a lower venue, academic-paper's answers to a low score, is not offered.
- A round-1 Reject kept at the round-2 stop: the user is offered accept for a draft academic-paper sends back with a roadmap.
- The literature search kept as ADR 0032 had it: the comparison's judges found a survey cited for its study, published sources labelled as preprints, a recency share below its target and a thin clinical side, and the simulation found a short count named after one widening.
- Scopus "Cited by" and Connected Papers reached through a browser: both need a bot check passed or scripts run in a browser, which the run never does.
- `refs.bib` kept in UTF-8: an entry with a character pdfLaTeX cannot set breaks the compile of `elsarticle`, `IEEEtran` and `acmart`.
- The citation check kept stopping at the first claim not held: the user rules on one claim, the check starts again, and the next claim not held stops it again.

## Consequences

A run may start a role more often than ADR 0027 counted, and the session chooses whether each later input resumes the agent or starts it fresh with its files. The draft review can end at a hand revision or a new venue, each followed by a fresh round-2 review numbered on, and a round-1 Reject can return the run to intake. A patch the script keeps rejecting can be retried without a cap or rewritten in full. The strategist runs more searches for a short count, a low currency share and each part of the question, and writes a synthesis by part. Each `refs.bib` entry is ASCII, and a `{?}` reaches the role's report. The citation check costs every claim's reading before its one stop. A full run leaves `main.pdf` and its DOCX or Markdown targets beside `main.tex`. `skills/academic/paper/SKILL.md`, its reference pages, `lookup.py` with its tests, `docs/academic-coverage.md`, the glossary entry "draft review", the README and the figures carry the change.
