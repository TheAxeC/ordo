# 0043. Paper's modes are academic-paper's, copied, with the deviations the goal and the rulings keep

Status: proposed

## Context

Paper's modes other than lit-review had grown a structure of their own beside academic-paper 3.2.0: a planner in place of the structure architect and the argument builder, the draft writer in place of the abstract agent, an intake agent in every mode, stops and agent starts academic-paper does not have, and pages that summarised academic-paper's files instead of holding them.

- Step 5.P made lit-review academic-paper's, with the strategist's page copied and the search left to the session (ADR 0042). In the rc.17 lit-review comparison the judges picked `paper` 4 of 4 against academic-paper (`.scratch/5-paper/agents/reviews/5.P-check-blind-comparison.md`, section "Lit-review").
- The same run took 3 h 30 min, 79 min over its bar, and no strategist's brief named a target number of sources (`.scratch/5-paper/agents/reviews/5.P-check-run.md`, section "The slower stage and its cause").
- The user ruled that every mode of `paper` is academic-paper's, copied and not rewritten, and that a deviation from academic-paper stands only where it makes the output better (`.scratch/5-paper/plan.md`, Rulings: "The rest of paper as academic-paper's", Open items 5.Q-rebuttal, 5.Q-letter-place, 5.Q held, 5.Q-target-modes, 5.Q-entry7 and AD16).

## Decision

1. Each file of academic-paper (`research-hub/.agents/skills/academic-paper/`) is copied into one page of `skills/academic/paper/`, section by section with the same words in the same order. Each page stands in the folder its source has in academic-paper, under the source's file name: a role's page is `agents/<role>_agent.md`, a reference page `references/<name>.md`, a template `templates/<name>` beside the scripts, and an example `examples/<name>.md`. Each can be read against its source by `diff` under the same name. The two pages with no source in academic-paper's folders, `external_review_protocol.md` and `revision_response_template.md`, stand in `references/`. Ten files are not copied: the three writing pages `references/academic_writing_style.md`, `references/writing_judgment_framework.md` and `references/writing_quality_check.md`, which `writing` holds; `references/changelog.md`; the Chinese-language and higher-education files `examples/chinese_paper_example.md`, `examples/imrad_hei_example.md`, `references/apa7_chinese_citation_guide.md`, `references/hei_domain_glossary.md` and `templates/bilingual_abstract_template.md`; and `examples/plan_mode_guided_writing.md`. The only edits are these:
   - `paper`'s names for academic-paper's files, roles and records;
   - a sentence on a role, a skill or a mechanism `paper` has no counterpart for, left out;
   - a reference to another academic-paper file named as the page that holds it;
   - the character set and the no-history rule of this repository;
   - the Chinese-language rules left out, the English text kept;
   - a rule that contradicts the manuscript prose standard left out, as ADR 0002 decides.
2. `skills/academic/paper/SKILL.md` is academic-paper's `SKILL.md`, each section carried with its words into the section of `docs/dev/skill-layout.md` that holds its kind of text. Version Info and Version History are left out.
3. Each mode's roles, agent starts, stops and cycles are academic-paper's: twelve roles, the intake agent only in full, outline-only, abstract-only, lit-review and plan, the outline approval, the Generator-Evaluator Contract's four calls in full mode, at most two reviews and one rewrite between them in full mode, two rounds in revision mode, and a Critical issue blocking Phase 7.

   A role's later task goes to the agent that holds the role's work, resumed with a message (ruling 5.Q-agent-starts). academic-paper's pages state no resumption of an agent. Resuming is `paper`'s own choice: a same-role fresh start makes the output no better and costs a re-read of every input and a re-fetch of every source. The resumed agents are these:
   - the draft writer, for the one rewrite of full mode's revision loop, the results-check issues, the reference fix, the compile fix, the findings pass and the response letter that ends a revision run;
   - the citation compliance agent, for its format work after the stop "The citation style to confirm", for the claims after the stop "A claim the run cannot support" and for each incremental check;
   - the Phase 6b peer reviewer, for the second review of full mode;
   - revision's peer reviewer, for the round-2 re-review and the letter's check.

   A new agent starts only for a role's first start in a run, for Phases 4a, 4b, 6a and 6b, and when the agent to resume is gone. The new agent is then given the files the gone agent wrote.

   These parts follow academic-paper (ruling 5.Q-leftovers):
   - Revision's re-review ends its loop with the items not resolved as acknowledged limitations and no further writer pass.
   - Phase 6b is given the contract's inputs alone.
   - A word count that deviates by 1% or more after a conversion is named as content to find and restore.
   - Plan mode suggests outline-only mode past 15 rounds.
   - Outside the stops its mode lists, a run takes the option that follows from its goal.

   Each part below is kept in its place in that structure, since the plan's goal requires it:
   - The LaTeX manuscript and its `.bib` are the manuscript's form, since the goal runs every mode on a LaTeX manuscript and its `.bib`.
   - Every change to the manuscript or its `.bib` is a patch applied by `anchorize_tex.py` and `apply_tex_patch.py`, since the goal moves the two scripts in for the revision patches.
   - The compile and its fix follow the patches, since a patch that breaks the LaTeX is otherwise found only by the user. The fix runs on a failed compile and on an overfull box of more than 10pt; a smaller box and a float too large start nothing (ruling 5.Q-leftovers).
   - Academic-paper's rewrite of the whole draft after the peer review is made as one patch, since every change is a patch.
   - The claim check against each source's full text runs in full, revision and citation-check mode, as the goal names it. In full and revision the intake row Claim check, default yes, decides it: with no, the citation compliance agent checks that each source exists and that its entry is in the citation style, reads no source's text, and still flags a claim of the row Claims not to make and a passage that repeats a paper of the row Earlier papers. Citation-check always runs it (ruling 5.Q-optional-checks).
   - The claim calibration with `calibration_counts.py` is kept, since it is part of the claim check.
   - The citation check of the cited sentences of each later patch is kept (ADR 0029), since a cited sentence a later patch adds is otherwise never checked and the claim check would not hold for the finished manuscript. The citation compliance agent of the first check is resumed for it. A finding of such a check never starts a writer pass: the agent's own `C<m>` patch closes it, and that patch gets no further check, or the report lists it, a drift item with suggested wording, and the final message names it (ruling 5.Q-check-loop). The intake row Incremental checks, default yes, decides whether these checks run, and they do not run when the row Claim check is no (ruling 5.Q-optional-checks).
   - The reference fix answers only a `missing:` line. A `check-urls` failure is named in the final message and in `checks.md` and starts nothing (ruling 5.Q-leftovers).
   - The `lookup.py` check of every reference the skill keeps, every other reference lookup and the `\cite` key check are kept (ADR 0017), as the goal names them.
   - Lit-review's identifier check is kept, since it is the `lookup.py` check of the references lit-review keeps.
   - The `/writing` review at the end of full and revision, with the findings the user accepts applied, is kept, since the goal applies the `/writing` findings the user accepts.
   - The draft writer's pass that judges and applies the `/writing` findings when the user asks is kept (ADR 0023), as the goal names it.
   - Intake's question whether full and revision end with `/writing` is kept (ADR 0031), asked by the session in revision, which has no intake agent, since the review is the user's choice.
   - Academic-paper's mode names stand under `/paper <mode>`, as the goal names them.
   - The run folder with `config.md` is kept (ADR 0020), since the records of these checks are written there.
   - The compile's engine is the first that applies of four: the engine a `% !TEX program =` line names, the engine the class table gives for the publisher class loaded, the engine the venue's template requires, and XeLaTeX (`references/latex_template_reference.md`, "The engine of the compile"). Only the three classes of that table take pdfLaTeX. The list is kept since each compile needs one engine named.
   The RAISE compliance agent is left out, since it is academic-pipeline's and comes with `researcher`. Every other choice of `paper` that academic-paper's text does not have is removed, except the deviations below, each kept for the reason given and under the ruling named:
   - Four refusals end a call before any agent starts: "Unknown mode", "An argument that does not exist", "The `.bib` is not one file" and "No prose standard". `docs/dev/skill-layout.md` asks that an input whose absence is a refusal says so; the third follows from the LaTeX manuscript and its `.bib`, and the fourth from Decision 4.
   - In the modes with no intake agent (revision, response-letter, format-convert, citation-check, disclosure, revision-coach, rebuttal-audit and the two calls) the session asks the user, in one message, only what the mode's items name, as academic-paper's modes without an intake agent take what they need from the call. Revision asks the rows Closing review, Claim check, Incremental checks, Claims not to make and Earlier papers, and citation-check the rows Claims not to make and Earlier papers; both ask where the study's result files and code are.
   - The intake row Claims not to make, asked in full and revision and by the session in citation-check, is read by the citation compliance agent, which flags each sentence that states such a claim (ruling Open item AI2), since academic-paper's claim intent manifest carries these negative constraints and the claim check otherwise does not see them.
   - The intake row Earlier papers, asked in full and revision and by the session in citation-check, is read by the citation compliance agent, which flags the passages that closely repeat the authors' earlier papers (ruling Open item AD8), as academic-paper's citation compliance agent does.
   - A negative or null result is never written up, with one exception: a manuscript the user asks for as internal only, which the intake row Internal only records and the intake confirmation shows (ruling Open item AT), since the user does not publish such a result and an internal record of every attempt is asked for by name.
   - The calls `draft-review` and `figures` run the peer reviewer and the visualization agent on an existing manuscript (ruling Open item AD14), as academic-paper's Phase-by-phase Invocation Contract runs one role per call.
   - A format error in more than half the cited entries repeats no claim or source check, a deviation from academic-paper's failure path F5, which re-runs the citation phase. The claim and source checks of the first start stand and its claim patches are written in every case. Entries in the style the Citation Format row names are corrected in the same start, as one patch, with no stop. Entries in another style raise the stop "The citation style to confirm" (ruling AV14), and after the answer the same agent is resumed for the format work only and reads no source again (ruling 5.Q-citation-restart). The restart repeated every claim and source check of the first start, and the question it answered concerns the entries' format only.
   - The results check: in full, revision and citation-check, when the user's materials include result files or code, the citation compliance agent reads each statement the manuscript makes about the study's own results, and each description of how the data were handled, against those files, whatever the row Claim check says. Its index in full mode is the trace table the draft writer writes in `draft-report.md`. In full and revision the draft writer is resumed once with the issues, as a patch with the ids `R<m>`; in citation-check they stand in the report. The peer reviewer is not given the files (ruling 5.Q-results-check). It is a deviation, since no check of academic-paper reads the study's own files, kept because two full runs described the handling of missing covariates in words the cohort file contradicts.
   - The stop "A reference that cannot be confirmed" is raised only under strict, as academic-paper's intake question gives it; under mark only the reference is marked in `citation-report.md` and named in the final message.
   - The response letter, its check and the mode `response-letter` stand as Decision 5 gives them (rulings Open item 5.Q-rebuttal and 5.Q-letter-place), since academic-paper has no step that answers referee comments with a letter and academic-pipeline's external review protocol gives both steps.
   - The intake row Number of sources stands as Decision 6 gives it (rulings Open item 5.Q held and 5.Q-target-modes), since no strategist's brief in the 5.P check run named a target number of sources and that run went 79 min over its bar.
   - The visualization agent starts beside Phase 4b, given `config.md`, `outline.md`, `arguments.md` and the file names of the manuscript's folder and its `figures/` folder, and draws the images, so that the figures are ready when the draft ends (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - Phase 4b is also given `refs.bib`, the Existing Materials and the model identifier, since the draft cites keys of the `.bib`, rests on the user's materials and names the model in its disclosure (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - The citation compliance agent also runs the predatory-venue screen, the checks of claims in clinical papers, the comparison of the claims against the writer's list in full mode and the check of the versions of one work, since each flags a venue, a claim or an entry the claim check would otherwise pass (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - An unused `.bib` entry is listed and never removed, since the reference list is the author's (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - Abstract-only and disclosure change the manuscript by a patch, with the ids `abstract` and `disclosure`, since every change to the manuscript is a patch and the text is asked for in the file (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - Format-convert writes a reference `.docx` with `reference_docx.py` when the user holds none and compiles each drawing to PNG, since `pandoc` needs both for a DOCX or Markdown target (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - The session checks each converted output by counts that two readers give alike, since a count is a fact a command computes (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - A `.bib` given to format-convert gets its reference list in the target's style with no formatter start, since the list is made by `pandoc` alone (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - A `.docx` given reaches no PDF target by any route, since a Word document is exported by its author (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - Intake offers the latest lit-review run it finds in `paper-runs/` as the user's own sources, since Checkpoint Rules 5 lets the user skip Phase 1 with own sources (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - The draft writer, the formatter and the citation compliance agent may run one web search for the page of a published version that `lookup.py bibtex` names, since the page's address is what `bibtex --page` needs for the entry (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - The first draft uses the publisher's class when the user holds no template and the venue is a journal of a listed publisher, since the venue's layout is then right from the first compile (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - A role other than the literature strategist writes no script and runs none of its own, except the visualization agent's figure scripts and the one command per statement of the results check, since a script computes only a fact and a role's work is judgment (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - A revision item that needs restructuring raises a stop of its own, "A revision item that needs restructuring", where the user chooses a full re-emission, which is recorded, or narrowed items, since academic-paper sends such a round to a mandatory user checkpoint (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - A source that could not be read stops nothing: `citation-report.md` lists it as not read with each address tried, and the final message names it. Only a claim its source's passage does not hold stops the check, since an unread source is no sign of fabrication (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - A structural patch the user refuses is narrowed once, and a narrowed patch that is structural again raises the stop again, since no change is dropped without the user's answer (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - The stop "The draft review does not pass" gives every option of academic-paper's peer reviewer page, (a) to (c) and A to C, since the choice after a failed second review is the user's (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - The session's one-message questions, the reply to the closing review, format-convert's two questions and the calibration's two questions are stops named in the Stops table, since a run stops only at a stop its mode lists (ruling Open item 5.R-audit).
   - Format-convert's formatter runs the pre-submission checks of `references/journal_submission_guide.md` for a journal venue and writes each with its result to `format-report.md`, the guide being copied as Decision 1 copies a page, since the goal of roadmap entry 5.A asks for it (rulings Submission checks folded in, Open item 4.A-copy and Open item 4.A-results).
4. The manuscript prose standard stands in every role's prompt (ADR 0026), and the strategist's page stays as step 5.P left it. Lit-review mode stays as step 5.P left it up to its last identifier check, and ends there with `review.md` and `refs.bib`, as academic-paper's agents 1 to 2 leave it: it makes no other target and starts no formatter (ruling Open item 5.R-source).
5. Revision-coach and rebuttal-audit are `paper`'s modes, with the revision coach's page and academic-paper's Rebuttal-Audit Mode section. Rebuttal-audit marks nothing as verified. Revision mode, when its comments are referee comments, ends with the response letter beside the revised manuscript, written by the run's draft writer, resumed, and its check by the run's peer reviewer, resumed, with a verdict for each point, as steps 3 and 4 of academic-pipeline's external review protocol give them. `/paper response-letter <main.tex> <comments file>` writes and checks the same letter for a revision already made, where the draft writer and the peer reviewer are first starts.
6. Intake has an optional row "Number of sources" in lit-review, full and outline-only mode. Given, the session splits it into one target range per literature strategist start, the ranges summing to the number.

## Alternatives rejected

- Keeping `paper`'s structure and running only the revision and full comparisons again: the structure that judges and the user had already ruled out for lit-review would stay in every other mode, and the result of such a comparison would not show which deviation made the difference.
- Widening step 5.P to every mode: 5.P was built and checked for lit-review alone, and its brief had not read the other modes against academic-paper.

## Consequences

- Each page of `skills/academic/paper/agents/`, `references/`, `templates/` and `examples/` can be read against the file of the same folder and name in academic-paper by `diff`, and a new deviation has to be argued against academic-paper's text with a run that shows it is better.
- `paper` has twelve modes and two calls. The pages `ai-disclosure.md`, `latex-template.md`, `modes.md`, `patch-file.md`, `planner.md` and `statements.md` are removed, and their content that the goal keeps stands as `paper`'s addition in the page of the source file whose step it serves.
- Removed from `paper`: the planner, the draft writer's abstract and statements, the intake agent of revision, format-convert, citation-check and disclosure, the unfinished-run stop, the stop on a research question the materials do not answer and the parts of the research question (ADR 0038), the approval of the revision items, the draft-review stops that academic-paper does not have, the anonymous copy, the palette outline, the one stop for every unsupported claim (ADR 0036), the peer reviewer's check of each number against the result files (ADR 0028) and the reading of the findings pass (ADR 0029), and the plan's handoff stop. A check against the result files stands as the results check of Decision 3, made by the citation compliance agent.
- A run starts fewer agents: each later task of a role costs one message to an agent that already holds the inputs and the fetched sources.
- `rebuttal`, roadmap entry 7, has no work left.
- The figures of `docs/figures/paper/` draw `paper` as built, and `docs/glossary.md` and `README.md` describe it.
