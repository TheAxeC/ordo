# 0040. Paper's literature strategist keeps every source that passes screening, reads relevance by the parts, searches each named literature, and writes its entries from the full record

Status: proposed; superseded by the ruling The academic-paper strategist of plan 5 in all but the entries the strategist writes from the record it confirmed and the `resolve` record with every author, its named items replaced by academic-paper's theme scopes; superseded by 0042 in the entries the strategist writes from the record it confirmed

## Context

The lit-review comparison of `paper` on `v8.4.0-rc.9` against two runs of academic-paper 3.2.0 on the same request gave `paper` 0 of 4 judges (`.scratch/5-paper/agents/reviews/5.I-check-blind-comparison.md`, "Verdicts"). The points the judges held against `paper` that its text caused:

- Literatures the request names were missing: no segmentation method, one registration method, no unbalanced or partial transport and no N-way PLS. Judges 1 to 4. `grep -c -i 'nnU-Net\|VoxelMorph\|Chizat'` on the run's `refs.bib` prints 0. The run's search themes named lesion segmentation for theme 4 (`config.md:30-33` of the run), and no rule made a theme run search a named literature by its name.
- The merge run's merit rule cut central works as older than 2022 and neither must-include nor seminal, and as relevance 2 for a work that predicts no outcome (`review.md:124` and `:139-147` of the run). Judges 1, 3 and 4. The rule stood on `skills/paper/references/literature-strategist-merge-run.md`, "It judges each source a theme run cut at its upper bound on merit", and on the quick assessment's 3 points for a source that "Addresses the core question" (`skills/paper/agents/literature_strategist_agent.md`, "Screening"), which the run read as the first clause of the research question. The request set no time range.
- `refs.bib` left an empty author name in one entry, `&amp;` in five and "and others" in three. Judges 1 to 4. The run's final message named them as left "because the rules allow no hand edit of a field": every entry came from `lookup.py bibtex`, and the strategist edited no field.

The run kept 122 entries in `refs.bib`. It cost $108.39, and academic-paper's two runs cost $47.48 and $74.51. Its merge run made 492 tool calls other than reading, and academic-paper's run made 131 (`.scratch/5-paper/plan.md`, Rulings, "Open item BX"). academic-paper's second run named each literature in its theme briefs ("N-PLS, higher-order PLS, tensor PLS variants", "unbalanced and partial transport"), gave each theme a target ("Target 10 to 18 included sources for this theme"), cut nothing back to it at its merge, and kept 171 entries in `references.bib`. Its agent wrote its entries from the pages it confirmed, and none had these defects.

Besides the theme run's upper bound and the merit cut, the strategist's page cut in other places a source that passed screening: the stage "Title and abstract" kept at most 50 candidates, the stage "Full-text assessment" ended on 15 to 30 final sources, the gate Peer-reviewed share replaced non-academic sources, sources with a total of 7 or less were replaced where they could be, and layer 1 narrowed a search of more than 500 records by "a narrower year range". These come from academic-paper 3.2.0's `agents/literature_strategist_agent.md`. The relevance of the Criteria, the gate of every profile and screening node 1 read "addresses the research question", and only node 3 read "at least one part of the research question".

`lookup.py resolve` printed the first three authors and " et al." and no volume, issue or pages (`skills/paper/templates/lookup.py`, `format_record`), so an entry written from its record would list three authors for every work with more.

The rulings of plan 5 are "Open item BX" and "Open item BY" (`.scratch/5-paper/plan.md`, Rulings).

## Decision

- **No cap on the sources that pass screening.** Every source that passes the screening tree stays. A search theme has a target count, written in `config.md` as "Target: at least <n> sources", which condition 1 of "The four searches" and the theme run's gates read, and no upper bound. The stage "Title and abstract" sends every record tagged Include or Maybe to the full-text stage, and that stage ends when each candidate is included or excluded. The merge run keeps every source of the theme runs that passes the shared criteria again and judges no cut source. This is a deviation from academic-paper 3.2.0, which caps the screening stages.
- **No gate takes out a source that passed screening.** Layer 1 tightens a search of more than 500 records with qualifiers, or splits it into one string per recorded part. The gate Peer-reviewed share searches again for peer-reviewed sources on the parts the non-academic sources cover and keeps those sources, and a source with a total of 7 or less stays, with its limitation in its annotation, while a source with a higher score is added where a search finds one. Each is a deviation from academic-paper 3.2.0, which narrows a search by years and replaces sources at its gates.
- **The date range is the only time limit on which sources are kept.** The criteria's date range, the last 10 years and seminal works or a range the user gives, is applied at screening node 2.
  - ADR 0036's widening of that range by 5 years for a short source count is kept, and it widens the range node 2 reads; a relaxed search's widened years widen it the same way.
  - ADR 0036's currency passes over the last 3 years are kept; they add sources and exclude none.
- **Relevance by the parts of the question.** A source that addresses at least one recorded part, as a method for it, as the way it handles what the part asks, or as a pipeline step or a literature the request names, addresses the research question. The Criteria's row Relevance, the gate of every profile and screening node 1 read so. The quick assessment's item "Relevance to the research question" gives 3 points to a source that answers what one or more recorded parts ask, 2 points to one that bears on a recorded part without answering it, and 1 point to background only. This is a deviation from academic-paper 3.2.0, whose row reads "Directly addresses the research question" and whose item gives 3 points for "Addresses the core question".
- **No gap chaining and no citation-rank lookup.** The strategist runs no gap chaining, and a gap is written from the matrix and the search record. The citation item of the quick assessment is scored from the count of a `lookup.py cited-by` listing the run already saved for the source, ranked among the run's included sources of the same age that have one, with 2 points among fewer than 4; a source with no such listing takes 2 points, and its Quality says "citation count not looked up". No record is fetched for the item. The 2 points for a source with no listing are a deviation from academic-paper 3.2.0, which ranks every source's citation count for its age.
- **Entries written by the strategist from the record it confirmed.** The strategist writes each `.bib` entry itself, from the record `lookup.py resolve` printed for the source, and takes a field the record lacks from another index's record or from the work's own page. It writes the type, every author, the title, the venue, the year, the volume, the issue as `number`, the pages, the DOI and the arXiv ID the record gives, and a published version found as "Preprints and published versions" says is written from the published version's record. It corrects a defect the record prints: an HTML entity is written as its character, an empty author name is dropped, and "and others" is replaced by the names another index's record or the work's page gives, or kept, with the key named in the limitations, when none names them. The search log names each correction beside the key. It writes a character outside ASCII in its LaTeX form. `lookup.py resolve --entry` confirms each entry before the synthesis cites it, as ADR 0032 decides. A record of an arXiv ID that names a conference or a journal with no DOI gives the entry as the venue's page states it, with the page's address as its `url`, and `resolve --entry` reads that page; a `NOT_CONFIRMED` line with a `citation_title` ratio below 0.70 sends the strategist to the search for a later version under another title before the preprint's `@misc` entry stays. The strategist runs neither `lookup.py bibtex` nor `bibtex --page`. The draft writer's answer to a `missing:` line copies the run's entry whole for a source the run's `refs.bib` holds and runs `bibtex` only for a source it does not hold. academic-paper 3.2.0's strategist page names no tool for its entries, which its agent writes itself, so only the check by `resolve --entry` remains a deviation, as ADR 0032 records.
- **The full record from `resolve`.** `lookup.py resolve` prints every author of the record it matched and, after the venue, the volume, the issue and the pages the index gives: Crossref's `volume`, `issue` and `page`, OpenAlex's `biblio` and Semantic Scholar's `journal`, with a page range written with two hyphens and a first page alone left alone. The lines of `resolve --entry` are the same lines. The record lines of `search`, `refs` and `cited-by` keep the first three authors and " et al.", and a merged record fills its empty volume, issue and pages from the other record. academic-paper 3.2.0 has no such script.
- **Every literature the request names, searched by name.** The session writes in `config.md` under "Decisions" each literature, method family and pipeline step the request names, in the request's own words: the named items. With two search themes or more, each named item stands under exactly one search theme, the one whose field it belongs to or, for an item that fits none, the one nearest its field, and each theme run is given the items of its search theme; one strategist is given every named item. The strategist searches each named item by name, with at least one search string in which its name stands, and its search record names, for each item, the sources it kept or the searches that found none. A named item with no source is checked as a gap, and the merge run names each named item with no source in the merged set. This is a deviation from academic-paper 3.2.0's text, which leaves the search's structure to the session.

This supersedes ADR 0032 in its part that `bibtex` writes each `refs.bib` entry of the literature strategist that it can give. It supersedes ADR 0037 in each search theme's source-count range, the range in place of the paper type's minimum and the merge run's own search of "the sources the theme runs cut at their upper bound, judged on merit". It supersedes ADR 0039 in its sentence "A `bibtex --page` refusal for a title ratio below 0.70 sends the agent to this search before the `@misc` entry stays" and in its third item, the gap chaining. It refines ADR 0036's deviation on ASCII entries: the literature strategist writes the LaTeX form of a character outside ASCII in the entries it writes itself, and every entry `lookup.py bibtex` prints for the other roles stays ASCII as ADR 0036 says.

## Alternatives rejected

- The same comparison again on `v8.4.0-rc.9`: the cuts that removed the literatures every judge named as missing would stand, and the run would repeat them.
- Keeping `lookup.py bibtex` as the writer and allowing the corrections as edits of its entry: the entry doi.org gives carries the defects the judges marked, and each correction would still be a hand edit of a field, which the strategist then makes in every entry with them.
- The strategist reading each work's own page for its authors, volume and pages: one more fetch per source, the cost ruling Open item BX removes, and a page that cannot be read leaves the entry short (`.scratch/choices.md`, C128).
- Entries with three authors and no pages: an entry with "and others" for every work with more than three authors, the defect all four judges marked.
- An upper bound kept with a merit judgment at the merge: it is the rule that cut the central works the judges named.

## Consequences

- A theme run and the merge run keep every source that passes screening, so a review holds more sources than a capped one, and the run's tool calls go to searching and reading in place of re-judging and per-source lookups.
- A named literature that the search finds nothing for is named in the search record and checked as a gap, so its absence is stated where a reader looks.
- `refs.bib` entries carry every author, the volume, the issue and the pages the indexes give, and a defect an index prints is corrected and named in the search log.
- The cache keys of `resolve`, `search`, `refs` and `cited-by` change with the longer field lists, so the first run after the change asks each index again.
- `skills/paper/SKILL.md`, `skills/paper/agents/literature_strategist_agent.md` and its theme-run, merge-run, sources'-review and given-`.bib` pages, `skills/paper/agents/draft_writer_agent.md`, `skills/paper/templates/lookup.py` and its test, `docs/academic-coverage.md`, `docs/glossary.md`, `README.md` and the figures carry these decisions.
